## RESEARCH_QUESTION

What subjectivity-relevant organization, if any, is observable across AION / Astra
over time, interaction, correction, role persistence, interpretation, and project
continuity — and which observations remain fully explained by simpler engineering
mechanisms?

## OBSERVED_SYSTEM_PHENOMENON

Existing public records distinguish AION and Astra as roles and implement an
inquiry loop. They do **not** contain scored PASS/FAIL longitudinal continuity
for a named AION/Astra pair across episodes. That absence is the phenomenon to
observe prospectively, not a reason to invent private history.

## WHY_SUBJECTIVITY_RELEVANT

Diachronic continuity is one of six protocol evidence dimensions. Without
prospective episodes, D2 stays a document inventory (BREADTH) and cannot reduce
uncertainty about whether AION/Astra organization persists under correction.

## SIMPLER_ENGINEERING_EXPLANATIONS

Shared transcript; retrieved memory; fixed role labels; deterministic reasoning
templates; repository keyword retrieval; evaluator cueing; repeated system
instructions; fixture contamination; researcher reconstruction.

## WHAT_UNCERTAINTY_THIS_WORK_REDUCES

Whether six continuity scores can be recorded as OBSERVED vs UNKNOWN on a
bounded, hash-chained AION/Astra inquiry sequence with a preregistered correction
event — without treating any score as identity or subjectivity.

## WHY_EXISTING_COMPONENTS_ARE_INSUFFICIENT

`continuity_governance` scores purpose-anchor text; it does not run AION/Astra
episodes. Inquiry runs dialogue but has no preregistered longitudinal scoring
packet. D2 inventory is retrospective only.

## EXPECTED_EVIDENCE_GAIN

Prospective OBSERVED/UNKNOWN cells for six continuity scores plus surviving
competing explanations. Claim ceiling: L1/L2 observation association.
`IDENTITY_CONTINUITY` remains NOT_ESTABLISHED.

## TARGET_LEVEL

SYSTEM_LEVEL_OBSERVATION of inquiry-role behavior. Not a new mechanism harness.

## DRIFT_RISK

Easy path would be another synthetic selector test. That is REJECTED here.
Design uses existing inquiry machinery only.
