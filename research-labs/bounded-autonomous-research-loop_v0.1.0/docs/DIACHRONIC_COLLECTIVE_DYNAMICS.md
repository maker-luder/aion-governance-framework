# Diachronic and Collective Dynamics Profile v0.1.0

## Placement

This additive profile is owned by the existing bounded autonomous research loop. It reuses its seven-state surface, operations, AION/Astra integration, Endogenous Goal Dynamics, Four-Domain type, subjectivity evidence matrix, and Evidence Interop path.

```text
DERIVED_OBSERVABLE != CANONICAL_STATE_CHANNEL
SEVEN_STATE_MODEL_REMAINS_EXACTLY_SEVEN = TRUE

```

No second loop, evidence format, Four-Domain framework, subjectivity score, or psychological state is introduced. The v0.1.0 contract remains bounded: this module derives additional observations without changing existing operations or reports.

## Research question

Can matched intervention, ablation, replay, and bounded counterfactual-proxy experiments distinguish narrow mechanisms underlying cross-time goal behavior, situation-sensitive strategy, role allocation, synthetic information routing, succession, and local/aggregate utility tradeoffs?

These are subjectivity-relevant probes, not evidence that a subject, experience, identity, collective mind, intention, or moral property exists.

Supportive narrow-mechanism observations require evidence and intervention refs, competing explanations, falsifiers, limitations, and intervention sensitivity.

## Subjectivity evidence mapping

The adapter produces the existing `SubjectivityEvidenceMatrix` with exactly the six standing dimensions. Temporal persistence, self/situation modeling, constraint response, and succession have narrow candidate mappings. Role specialization, information disclosure, and local/collective tradeoff default to not applicable instead of being forced into the matrix. Existing intervention-sensitive causal support and self-report exclusions remain authoritative.

## AION/Astra, Four-Domain, and provenance

The inquiry bundle carries exact evidence refs, state and perturbation fingerprints, competing explanations, falsifiers, limitations, and explicit anthropomorphic challenges. Initial peer transcript/evidence exposure and direct communication flags are false. Peer agreement cannot upgrade scientific status.

The Four-Domain adapter returns the current EGD `FourDomainMapping`; it does not create Four-Domain v2. The evidence extension binds exact commit, protocol hash, state/perturbation/matrix fingerprints, dispositions, refs, alternatives, and limitations for reuse through the existing evidence and Evidence Interop path. It is not a second evidence schema.
