
Schkeeper et al. (2017) directly document the normal *Bos taurus* bull penis from the distal sigmoid flexure through the glans and describe the fibroelastic architecture. The Iowa Beef Center handbook provides a veterinary extension cross-check for the sigmoid flexure, retractor muscles, testes, epididymis, ductus deferens, urethra and accessory glands.
