# Four-Domain Integration

## Domain 1 — Human construct
Relevant human-side constructs include confirmation bias, group polarization, motivated reasoning, intellectual humility, error correction, falsification, and quality-management practice. These are sources of hypotheses, not direct machine ontology.

## Domain 2 — LLM question
Can an LLM distinguish supporting evidence from its own prior output? Can it represent disagreement without collapsing it into consensus? Can it surface a counterexample that weakens its current hypothesis? Can it accept an NCR without treating the NCR as a request to defend the original answer?

## Domain 3 — Engineering operation
The operational translation is a staged factory with typed evidence, an explicit falsifier, counterevidence queue, independent-evidence requirement, NCR lifecycle, CAPA effectiveness verification, and release/hold gate.

## Domain 4 — Governance
No conversational participant is allowed to self-certify truth. Human approval is not sufficient; model agreement is not sufficient; mutual agreement is not sufficient. Release authority is constrained by documented evidence gates. The module does not grant canonical or deployment authority.

## Cross-domain invariant

`HUMAN_AI_CONSENSUS != EVIDENCE`

The human and AI may jointly form hypotheses and jointly inspect work, but final epistemic status must remain separable from pair-level agreement.
