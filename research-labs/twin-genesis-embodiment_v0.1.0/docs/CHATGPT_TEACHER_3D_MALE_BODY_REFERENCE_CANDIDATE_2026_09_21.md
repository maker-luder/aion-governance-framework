# Skeletal and articulation requirements

A future rig should represent a conventional humanoid skeletal hierarchy with sufficient articulation for realistic teaching and conversational gestures.

Required articulation groups:

- cervical, thoracic, and lumbar spine;
- clavicle and scapular motion;
- shoulder ball-and-socket motion;
- elbow flexion / extension and forearm pronation / supination;
- wrist flexion, extension, radial and ulnar deviation;
- all finger metacarpal / phalangeal chains;
- pelvis and sacroiliac approximation;
- hip flexion / extension, abduction / adduction, rotation;
- knee flexion / extension;
- ankle dorsiflexion / plantarflexion and inversion / eversion approximation;
- all toe chains;
- jaw;
- eyes;
- brows and facial-expression controls.

# Candidate deformation tests

## TEST-001 — Teaching gesture

```text
POSE = STANDING_ONE_ARM_FORWARD_OPEN_HAND
PURPOSE = EXPLANATION_GESTURE
```

Pass concepts:

- shoulder / scapula remain plausible;
- axillary topology does not collapse;
- elbow and wrist bend without pinching;
- fingers remain independently controllable;
- chest and clothing-contact surfaces remain coherent.

## TEST-002 — Seated discussion

```text
POSE = SEATED_HIPS_AND_KNEES_FLEXED
PURPOSE = CONVERSATIONAL_POSTURE
```

Pass concepts:

- abdomen compresses naturally;
- pelvis and gluteal tissue show load-bearing deformation;
- groin topology remains continuous;
- thighs do not intersect the torso;
- external genital anatomy follows gravity / contact as neutral soft tissue;
- knees do not form a single rigid hinge crease;
- feet retain floor contact.

## TEST-003 — Walking

```text
POSE = MID_GAIT
PURPOSE = GENERAL_LOCOMOTION_REFERENCE
```

Pass concepts:

- pelvis rotates plausibly;
- contralateral arm swing is supported;
- hip / thigh topology remains continuous;
- knees and ankles deform without collapse;
- foot roll is representable.
