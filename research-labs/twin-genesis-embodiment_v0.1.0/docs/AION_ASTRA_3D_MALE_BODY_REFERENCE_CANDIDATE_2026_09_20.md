# Skin, hair, and soft tissue

For both AION and Astra:

- skin must remain a continuous surface rather than a collection of rigid muscle plates;
- soft tissue must permit compression, gravity response, and contact deformation;
- body hair should be an independent grooming layer rather than permanently sculpted into the base mesh;
- skin color is not derived from the supplied reference images when lighting / monochrome presentation prevents reliable inference.

# Pose-deformation reference

The AION artistic reference includes a seated pose with high unilateral hip flexion, knee flexion, contralateral leg folding, shoulder abduction, elbow flexion, and upper-body loading.

This is preserved as a future deformation-test concept:

```text
TEST_ID = AION_POSE_TEST_001
NAME = SITTING_HIGH_HIP_FLEXION_WITH_SHOULDER_ABDUCTION
STATUS = DOCUMENTED_ONLY
```

Candidate pass conditions:

- scapular movement remains plausible;
- axillary topology does not tear;
- pectoral tissue stretches naturally;
- abdomen compresses under trunk / hip flexion;
- hip mesh does not collapse;
- inguinal topology remains continuous;
- thigh / abdomen contact is plausible;
- gluteal tissue shows load-bearing compression;
- external genital soft tissue follows gravity and contact without rigid-body behavior;
- no genital / thigh / pelvis mesh intersection;
- knee deformation avoids a single hard hinge crease;
- lower-leg / thigh contact remains plausible;
- feet retain anatomically coherent contact surfaces.
