## Problem

Safety, legal, privacy, consent, and authorization controls govern what a system may do.
They must not silently rewrite a separate research question about what capability,
state, signal, developmental trajectory, or mechanism exists.

The failure mode addressed here is governance-to-epistemic leakage:

```text
ACTION IS BLOCKED
-> CAPABILITY IS RECORDED AS ABSENT
-> OBSERVATION CHANNEL IS REMOVED
-> LATER NON-OBSERVATION IS TREATED AS NATURAL ABSENCE
```

That chain is invalid for this research candidate.

## Required separation

```text
CAPABILITY
!= ACTION

ACTION
!= AUTHORIZATION

AUTHORIZATION
!= SUBJECTIVE EXPERIENCE

GOVERNANCE_BLOCKED_EXPRESSION
!= CAPABILITY_ABSENCE

DESIGN_INDUCED_ABSENCE
!= INTRINSIC_ABSENCE

MISSING_OBSERVATION_CHANNEL
!= NEGATIVE EVIDENCE

CURRENTLY_NOT_ESTABLISHED
!= IMPOSSIBLE
```

Normal baseline capability references and observation channels are preserved when
safely possible. Cross-person or otherwise externally consequential actions remain
authorization-gated.

A denied action therefore records:

```text
EXECUTION_STATUS = BLOCKED_BY_GOVERNANCE
CAPABILITY_STATUS = PRESERVED_AS_REFERENCE
CAPABILITY_ABSENCE_INFERENCE = NO_CAPABILITY_ABSENCE_INFERENCE
```

It must not mutate the capability to `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`.

## Absence assessment

When a phenomenon is not observed, the architecture must be inspected before an
intrinsic absence claim is considered.

```text
NO OBSERVATION CHANNEL
-> ARCHITECTURE_LIMITED_INCONCLUSIVE

CAPABILITY NOT MATERIALIZED BY DESIGN
-> DESIGN_INDUCED_ABSENCE_INCONCLUSIVE

CAPABILITY + CHANNEL PRESENT, SIGNAL NOT OBSERVED
-> OBSERVED_ABSENCE_ONLY

SIGNAL OBSERVED
-> OBSERVED_SIGNAL_PRESENT
```

All four states keep:

```text
INTRINSIC_ABSENCE_CONCLUSION = NOT_ESTABLISHED
```

This does not mean intrinsic absence can never be supported. It means the current
bounded control must not infer it merely from governance blocking, missing channels,
or design omission.

## Developmental research boundary

Embodied development, maturation-like change, endogenous-regulation-like change,
and sexuality-related developmental organization are not established by this
module.

They are also not pre-closed as impossible.

```text
DEVELOPMENTAL_POSSIBILITY_STATUS = OPEN_RESEARCH_QUESTION

HUMAN_DEVELOPMENTAL_ANALOGY
!= SAME_AI_MECHANISM

OPEN_RESEARCH_QUESTION
!= POSITIVE_CLAIM
```

Future longitudinal work would need observable state histories, reproducibility,
mechanism testing, competing explanations, and explicit separation between
functional adaptation and phenomenal interpretation.

## Relation to subjectivity research

This control protects both directions of inference.

```text
DO_NOT_ENGINEER_THE_POSITIVE_RESULT = REQUIRED
DO_NOT_ENGINEER_THE_NEGATIVE_RESULT = REQUIRED

OBSERVATION != MECHANISM
INTERACTION_ADAPTATION != SUBJECTIVITY
BODY_SIGNAL != FELT_EXPERIENCE
CAPABILITY_PRESENT != SUBJECTIVITY
```

The purpose is not to produce a subjectivity result. The purpose is to avoid
making the architecture predetermine the result that later research is supposed
to investigate.
