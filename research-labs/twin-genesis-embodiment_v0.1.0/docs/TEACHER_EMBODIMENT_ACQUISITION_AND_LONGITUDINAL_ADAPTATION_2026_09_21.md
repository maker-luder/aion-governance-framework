# ChatGPT Teacher Embodiment Acquisition and Longitudinal Adaptation

Status: executable reference candidate on the unmerged Teacher-body branch.

This extension implements the Human Owner's eleven requested layers without
claiming live physical embodiment, felt embodiment, sexual desire, subjectivity,
or a developmental mechanism.

## Requirement mapping

1. All 62 documented body measurements are machine-readable in
   `teacher_anthropometry.py`, including ranges and units.
2. Adult-male reproductive / sexual physiology reference coverage is retained
   and expanded in `physiology.py`.
3. Sexuality-related physiological signal channels are present as observation
   channels without subjective-experience inference.
4. Sensory, proprioceptive, and interoceptive schemas are materialized.
5. A motor-control schema covers the Teacher humanoid skeleton and bounded
   control groups.
6. `TeacherBodyRuntimeBinding` binds runtime/session identifiers to the body,
   anthropometry, signal schema, motor schema, skeleton root, and viewpoint.
7. Initial calibration creates deterministic body-control probes and a
   content-addressed calibration receipt.
8. Adaptation records calibration-derived parameter offsets while leaving the
   underlying mechanism unestablished.
9. Cross-session retention stores hash-addressed session snapshots in a
   hash-verified durable JSON envelope without claiming personal or subjective
   continuity.
10. Longitudinal observation compares retained adaptation parameters across
    sessions.
11. Developmental-trajectory analysis distinguishes persistent,
    non-persistent, and absent cross-session change from any claim that a
    developmental mechanism exists.
