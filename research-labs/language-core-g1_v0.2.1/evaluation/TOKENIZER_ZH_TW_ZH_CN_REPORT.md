## Offline contract fixture — 2026-08-14 sandbox update

It also records a non-streaming local Ollama telemetry contract in nanoseconds and representative malformed payloads for fail-closed parser tests.

The fixture status is `OFFLINE_CONTRACT_ONLY`: it does not load model weights, execute a tokenizer, produce benchmark scores, validate streaming parity, or support a capability or scientific conclusion. The existing `TOKENIZER_ZH_TW_ZH_CN_METRICS.json` therefore remains `NOT_EXECUTED_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE` with `metrics = null`.
