# QA admission gates

Gates 1–10 cover lineage, source/license, hash, baseline evidence, target effect, side effects, Traditional Chinese, instruction following, reasoning/coding and human approval. Missing thresholds or human approval produce `QA_HOLD`.

`APPROVED_CAPABILITY_ARTIFACT` additionally requires explicit artifact identity, scope, evidence, rollback, security, integrity, Runtime level and Owner approval. Approval grants no identity, subjectivity, memory, tool, canonical, deployment or public-release authority.

Research Proposal registration is not an admission gate result. Every current proposal remains `NOT_STARTED / QA_HOLD / canonical_effect=NONE`.
