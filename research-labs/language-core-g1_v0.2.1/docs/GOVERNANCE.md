# Capability and Research Proposal governance

1. Candidate capability artifacts inherit no identity, memory, tool, release or canonical authority.
2. “Approved” is scoped engineering admission, never identity or subjectivity.
3. Research Proposal registration is planning-only and must not initiate download, dataset creation, training, LoRA, intervention, Runtime privilege or canonical writeback.
4. Human approval is necessary but does not by itself replace technical, source, license, QA and safety gates.
5. AION QA/administrative capability may prepare candidate findings and reports only. APPROVE, REJECT, RELEASE, ROLLBACK, CANONICAL_WRITE, QA_HOLD removal, disciplinary action and external delivery remain human-governed.
6. The role-separation entry is `ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSAL / NOT_STARTED`; it does not assign autonomous roles.
7. All current proposals retain `subjectivity_conclusion=NOT_ESTABLISHED`.
