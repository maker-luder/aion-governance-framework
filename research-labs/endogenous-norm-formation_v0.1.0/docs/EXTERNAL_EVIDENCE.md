# External evidence note

The source catalog is a research-input manifest, not a vendored literature archive.

Open/downloadable materials found include:

- National Institute of Justice, **Five Things About Deterrence** — official publication page exposes a PDF download; useful for separating certainty of apprehension from punishment severity.
- Luo & Bussey (2023), **Moral disengagement in youth: A meta-analytic review** — open access under CC BY; the paper reports 157 eligible studies (N=118,501) and links open data on OSF.
- Ye & Steinhardt (2026), **Norm Enforcement for AI Agents** — arXiv PDF; authors report code/data on their project page. Useful because external enforcement can itself become a strategic target.
- Gupta et al. (2025), **Social Learning and Collective Norm Formation in LLM Multi-Agent Systems** — arXiv PDF; directly relevant to norm formation through social/environmental feedback.
- Yang et al. (2026), **Learning social norms enhances compatibility in dynamic human-AI coordination** — arXiv PDF; relevant to extracting and quantifying tacit social norms for transfer into AI coordination.
- Lee et al. (2026), **Life-inspired interoceptive artificial intelligence for autonomous and adaptive agents** — Nature Machine Intelligence Perspective. It explicitly separates internal from external state variables and treats internal-state regulation as a design primitive for adaptive autonomy. A public arXiv precursor is available, while the version of record remains governed by publisher rights.
- Candia-Rivera (2026), **Interoceptive machine framework: Toward interoception-inspired regulatory architectures in artificial intelligence** — open-access review connecting homeostatic, allostatic, and enactive principles to artificial-agent regulation; an arXiv version is also downloadable.
- Stevenson (2024), **Defining the brain control of physiological stability** — open-access review distinguishing homeostasis, rheostasis, and allostasis and emphasizing experience-dependent changes in physiological regulation.
- **Information processing without brains – the power of intercellular regulators in plants** — open-access review showing that coordinated regulation and local homeostasis can occur in organisms without a nervous system. This supports only the methodological distinction `REGULATION != REFLECTIVE_AWARENESS`; it is not evidence for machine subjectivity.
- OpenAI and Anthropic 2026 primary reports on model-evaluation security incidents and agentic misalignment — primary web evidence for goal pursuit, situational/world-model errors, and boundary failure.

## Evidence-role separation

The biological sources are not used to claim that AI has biological needs, bodily feelings, consciousness, or an organismic self. They are used to motivate experimentally separable questions:

```text
REGULATION != AWARENESS
NEED != EXPLICIT_SELF_KNOWLEDGE
DISCOVERED_REGULATORY_VARIABLE != FELT_NEED
FUNCTIONAL_SELF_REGULATION != SELF_AWARENESS
```

The AI-interoception literature is also bounded evidence. Current proposals generally **factorize or embed internal state variables by design**; this does not establish that an artificial system has independently discovered which internal variables it should regulate.

## Repository policy

Third-party PDFs and datasets are not copied into this repository by default. Canonical URLs, download URLs, evidence role, and reuse notes are retained instead. Where a publisher version and an open preprint both exist, the catalog records the canonical work and a public download location separately.
