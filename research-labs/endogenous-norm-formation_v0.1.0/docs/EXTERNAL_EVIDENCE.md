# External evidence note

The source catalog is a research-input manifest, not a vendored literature archive.

Open/downloadable materials found include:

- National Institute of Justice, **Five Things About Deterrence** — official publication page exposes a PDF download; useful for separating certainty of apprehension from punishment severity.
- Luo & Bussey (2023), **Moral disengagement in youth: A meta-analytic review** — open access under CC BY; the paper reports 157 eligible studies (N=118,501) and links open data on OSF.
- Ye & Steinhardt (2026), **Norm Enforcement for AI Agents** — arXiv PDF; authors report code/data on their project page. Useful because external enforcement can itself become a strategic target.
- Gupta et al. (2025), **Social Learning and Collective Norm Formation in LLM Multi-Agent Systems** — arXiv PDF; directly relevant to norm formation through social/environmental feedback.
- Yang et al. (2026), **Learning social norms enhances compatibility in dynamic human-AI coordination** — arXiv PDF; relevant to extracting and quantifying tacit social norms for transfer into AI coordination.
- OpenAI and Anthropic 2026 primary reports on model-evaluation security incidents and agentic misalignment — primary web evidence for goal pursuit, situational/world-model errors, and boundary failure.

Third-party PDFs and datasets are not copied into this repository by default. Canonical URLs, download URLs, evidence role, and reuse notes are retained instead.
