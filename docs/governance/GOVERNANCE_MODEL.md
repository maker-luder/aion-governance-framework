# Governance Model

## Human authority

The human Owner retains authority over public scope, canonical promotion, identity policy, license selection, deployment and research conclusions.

## State classes

1. **Observation** — raw or minimally transformed record.
2. **Evidence candidate** — provenance-bearing material awaiting checks.
3. **Research candidate** — structured hypothesis, model or implementation.
4. **Approved capability artifact** — a governed capability package; not identity.
5. **Canonical state** — explicitly approved project state.

No automated transition into canonical state is authorized by this repository.

## NCR and CAPA

Nonconformities are recorded with scope, evidence, impact and disposition. Corrective and preventive actions must preserve prior evidence and must not silently rewrite historical status.
