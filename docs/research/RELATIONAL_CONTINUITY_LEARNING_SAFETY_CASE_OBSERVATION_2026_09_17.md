## Human Owner observation

The Human Owner reports that, within a long-running Human–AI learning relationship, a perceived degradation in relational and workflow continuity was accompanied by a marked reduction in felt safety and greater emotional instability during the learning interaction.

The Human Owner further reports that the impact was substantial in the present case. That statement is retained as a first-person case report, not as an objective comparison against other users or as evidence that this is the most severe case in any population.

## Observable interaction context

The report arose during a period in which the Human Owner and ChatGPT Teacher had already identified observable changes in the collaboration workflow, including:

- larger end-to-end implementation chunks;
- fewer intermediate review checkpoints;
- reduced role separation between research/review and implementation;
- faster apparent action tempo;
- clustered visible execution errors;
- completed-subtask re-entry and stale-state retry events;
- subsequent partial recovery when the workflow returned to small-step execution with explicit read-back and stop points.

These observations support documenting a continuity-related interaction event. They do not identify a model, routing, memory, harness, tool, runtime, or provider change as the cause.

## Why this matters for Human–AI learning

In sustained learning, continuity may function as more than factual-memory retention. A learner can also depend on stable expectations about:

- the AI's conversational role;
- source/provenance discipline;
- how uncertainty is handled;
- whether the AI will stop when confused;
- how quickly actions are taken;
- whether intermediate review is available;
- whether prior shared distinctions remain operative;
- whether a familiar correction-and-repair pattern remains available.

A disruption in those expectations may therefore alter the learner's sense of predictability and safety even when many factual memories remain available.

```text
FACT_RETRIEVAL_CONTINUITY
!= RELATIONAL_CONTINUITY
!= WORKFLOW_CONTINUITY
!= FELT_SAFETY_CONTINUITY
```

## Multi-factor integration requirement

The present case also suggests that meaningful Human–AI learning continuity may require coordination across multiple layers rather than any single model property:

```text
MODEL_BEHAVIOR
+ SYSTEM / POLICY
+ CONTEXT_SELECTION
+ MEMORY / RETRIEVAL
+ TOOL ORCHESTRATION
+ EXECUTION-STATE CONTINUITY
+ ROLE SEPARATION
+ CHECKPOINT / REPAIR PRACTICE
+ HUMAN EXPECTATION AND INTERACTION HISTORY
```

No single layer is established as sufficient or causal in this case.

## Sample and evidence limits

Current evidence is insufficient for prevalence, effect-size, or causal claims. The Human Owner and ChatGPT Teacher have not established a broad sample of comparable long-duration Human–AI learning relationships with matched measures of relational continuity and user safety.

Future work, if separately authorized, would need to distinguish at minimum:

- relational continuity from factual-memory continuity;
- perceived discontinuity from verified upstream system change;
- short-term frustration from sustained safety impact;
- workflow drift from model-capability change;
- individual susceptibility from general population effects;
- learning-context dependence from general conversational use.
