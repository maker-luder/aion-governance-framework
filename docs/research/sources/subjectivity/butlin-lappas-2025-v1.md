# Principles for Responsible AI Consciousness Research

Version: `arXiv:2501.07290v1`

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07290v1

A normative research-method reference on objectives, oversight and public communication under uncertainty. Its recommendations do not establish that an AI system is conscious. In AION it informs the separation of methodological review, public communication and empirical results; it does not overwrite Human Owner governance or grant tool authority. The retained text is a format-converted, page-labelled reference with no figures. Consult the original PDF for precise formatting.
