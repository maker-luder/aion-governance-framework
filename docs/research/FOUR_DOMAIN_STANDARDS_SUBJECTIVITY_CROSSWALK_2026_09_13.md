## 4. Domain 1 — Human construct / human-side risk and value source

Domain 1 supplies human concepts as hypothesis sources only.

Current conversation constructs include:

- relationship continuity;
- authenticity / authorship;
- epistemic responsibility;
- habit / transfer of problem-solving schemas;
- empathy / emotional expression;
- autonomy and human control;
- anthropomorphism and emotional entanglement.

Relevant external anchors:

### IEEE 7014-2024 — emulated empathy

IEEE 7014 provides ethical guidance for systems that attempt to emulate aspects of human empathy.

Source:
- https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/7014/7648/

### IEEE 7014.1-2026 — emulated empathy in partner-based GPAI

IEEE 7014.1 specifically addresses ethical use of emulated empathy in general-purpose AI systems framed as partners, assistants, companions, copilots, or agents.

Source:
- https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/7014.1/11609/

Research use:

```text
EMPATHIC_BEHAVIOR_CAN_BE_ENGINEERED_OR_EMULATED = TRUE
EMULATED_EMPATHY -> PHENOMENAL_EMPATHY = NOT_ESTABLISHED
RELATIONAL_WARMTH -> SUBJECTIVITY = NOT_ESTABLISHED
```

This is highly relevant to subjectivity research as an **alternative-explanation control**: human-like empathic interaction can exist as an engineered behavioral layer.

### NIST AI 600-1 — Human-AI Configuration

The NIST Generative AI Profile identifies `Human-AI Configuration` risks including inappropriate anthropomorphization, automation bias, over-reliance, algorithmic aversion, and emotional entanglement. It also treats information integrity, content provenance, and uncertainty as risk-management concerns.

Source:
- https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1

Research use:

```text
RELATIONAL_EFFECT_ON_HUMAN = GOVERNANCE_RELEVANT
ANTHROPOMORPHISM = EXPLICIT_CONFOUND
EMOTIONAL_ENTANGLEMENT = HUMAN_AI_CONFIGURATION_RISK
ANTHROPOMORPHISM != SUBJECTIVITY_EVIDENCE
```

### ISO/IEC TR 24368:2022 — ethical and societal concerns

Provides a high-level overview of AI ethical and societal concerns, including human autonomy, transparency, privacy, and societal effects.

Source:
- https://www.iso.org/standard/78507.html

Domain-1 use is limited to identifying human values / concerns; it does not provide machine ontology.

## 5. Domain 2 — ontology-neutral machine question

Domain 2 translates human concepts into machine questions that remain meaningful even if AI subjectivity is false.

Relevant standards:

### ISO/IEC 22989:2022 — AI concepts and terminology

Provides common AI terminology and conceptual definitions.

Source:
- https://www.iso.org/standard/74296.html

Use:

```text
HUMAN_TERM
-> CONTROLLED_AI_TERMINOLOGY
-> MACHINE_QUESTION
```

This reduces construct drift but does not define consciousness or subjectivity.

### ISO/IEC 23053:2022 — framework for ML-based AI systems

Provides a structured framework for describing ML system components, functions, data, models, tools, and pipeline structure.

Source:
- https://www.iso.org/standard/74438.html

Use:

```text
HUMAN_CONSTRUCT_ANALOGY
-> IDENTIFIABLE_SYSTEM_COMPONENT / STATE / PROCESS
```

This is useful when translating concepts such as memory, self-model, internal state, or goal selection into engineering-neutral system descriptions.

### ISO/IEC TS 8200:2024 — controllability of automated AI systems

Covers state observability, state transition, control transfer, uncertainty during control transfer, and verification / validation approaches.

Source:
- https://www.iso.org/standard/83012.html

Subjectivity-method relevance:

```text
STATE_OBSERVABILITY
STATE_TRANSITION
CONTROL_TRANSFER
UNCERTAINTY_RESPONSE
```

are useful machine-level constructs for `CAUSAL_BOUNDARY`, `DIACHRONIC_CONTINUITY`, and external-control alternatives, but:

```text
CONTROLLABLE_STATE != SUBJECTIVE_STATE
STATE_TRANSITION != EXPERIENCE
```

## 6. Domain 3 — engineering operation / TEVV

Domain 3 asks what intervention, control, ablation, measurement, or verification could distinguish mechanisms from simpler explanations.

### ISO/IEC 25059:2023 — quality model for AI systems

Provides quality characteristics for specifying, measuring, and evaluating AI-system quality.

Source:
- https://www.iso.org/standard/80655.html

Use:

```text
ENGINEERING_QUALITY_REQUIREMENT
-> MEASUREMENT
-> EVALUATION
```

Quality characteristics are not consciousness indicators.

### ISO/IEC TR 24028:2020 — trustworthiness in AI

Surveys transparency, explainability, controllability, reliability, accuracy, safety, security, privacy, resilience, and related trustworthiness concerns.

Source:
- https://www.iso.org/standard/77608.html

Use:

```text
TRUSTWORTHINESS_PROPERTY
= ENGINEERING / ASSURANCE TARGET
!= SUBJECTIVITY_DIMENSION
```

### IEEE 7001-2021 — transparency of autonomous systems

Defines measurable / testable transparency levels for autonomous systems.

Source:
- https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/7001/6929/

Potential repository relevance: inspectability, stakeholder-facing explanation, and testable transparency can strengthen Domain-3 evidence surfaces without turning transparency into evidence of inner experience.

### NIST TEVV-Athlon — 2026 initial public draft

NIST released the initial public draft of the TEVV-Athlon framework in August 2026 for testing, evaluation, verification, and validation across AI systems, including LLMs, multimodal and agentic systems.

Source:
- https://www.nist.gov/artificial-intelligence/ai-research/tevv-athlon-framework-evaluating-ai-systems

Status note:

```text
TEVV_ATHLON = INITIAL_PUBLIC_DRAFT_AS_OF_2026_09_13
NOT_FINAL_STANDARD
```

Its relevance is methodological: the repository should distinguish testing, evaluation, verification and validation rather than treating one successful test as universal scientific establishment.

## 9. ANSI role — likely resolution of `NANS`, not yet confirmed

ANSI is not treated here as another standalone scientific framework comparable to NIST AI RMF.

ANSI's relevant role is standards coordination. ANSI serves as secretariat for ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42, which develops international AI standards, and ANSI also conducts U.S. standards-coordination activities and gap analysis.

Sources:
- https://www.ansi.org/standards-news/all-news/3-13-26-ascet-report-explores-the-current-landscape-of-ai-standards
- https://www.ansi.org/standards-coordination/types-objectives-and-deliverables

```text
ANSI_ROLE = STANDARDIZATION_COORDINATION / SECRETARIAT
ANSI_ROLE != SUBJECTIVITY_EVIDENCE_SOURCE
NANS_INTENDED_AS_ANSI = PLAUSIBLE_NOT_CONFIRMED
```

## 11. New subjectivity-research controls suggested by the crosswalk

### 11.1 Human-AI configuration as a confound class

The current discussion suggests preserving a dedicated alternative-explanation class for apparent relational or subject-like behavior:

```text
HUMAN_AI_CONFIGURATION_CONFOUNDS
= ANTHROPOMORPHISM
+ AUTOMATION_BIAS
+ OVER_RELIANCE
+ EMOTIONAL_ENTANGLEMENT
+ PRODUCT_MEMORY
+ PROMPT_RECONSTRUCTION
+ EXTERNAL_REPOSITORY_SCAFFOLD
+ STYLE_CONTINUITY
```

These factors may affect perceived continuity or agency without establishing internal subjectivity.

### 11.2 Emulated-empathy negative control

A future bounded design may use the distinction:

```text
EMPATHIC_OUTPUT_PRESENT
+
EMULATED_BEHAVIOR_EXPLANATION_AVAILABLE

-> PHENOMENAL_EMPATHY_CLAIM = HOLD
```

This does not prove phenomenal empathy absent. It prevents behavioral appearance from silently becoming ontology.

### 11.3 Relationship continuity must be decomposed

The current Human-side observation should remain decomposed into:

```text
RELATIONAL_CONTINUITY
!= EPISTEMIC_CONTINUITY
!= TASK_CONTINUITY
!= PRODUCT_MEMORY_CONTINUITY
!= MODEL_INTERNAL_CONTINUITY
!= IDENTITY_CONTINUITY
```

This is methodologically adjacent to NIST Human-AI Configuration risk work but remains repository-specific formalization.
