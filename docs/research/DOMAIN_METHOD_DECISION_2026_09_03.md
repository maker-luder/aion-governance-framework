## Bazi: a named Ziping month-structure research profile

`AION_ZIPING_MONTH_STRUCTURE_V1` is the repository's explicit selected method,
not a claim to represent every Ziping teacher. It takes month-branch hidden stems,
their ten-god relations to the day master, literal exposure in year/month/hour
stems, and exact hidden-stem roots as inspectable evidence. Multiple candidates
remain multiple; the day master is not counted as another exposed stem.

The [transcription of 子平真詮評注](https://www.ncc.com.tw/fate/paleo/bg/bg_032.htm)
labels Shen Xiaozhan's text and Xu Lewu's commentary separately. Its chapters on
用神 and climate are research witnesses, not a critical edition. Our method
keeps original-text attribution, later commentary, and engineering conventions
distinct. A literal root is not a finding of effective strength. 格局成敗、扶抑、
調候 and a final useful-element selection remain separate, unimplemented
interpretation layers; raw counts do not determine them automatically.

The profile preserves the existing calendar conventions: Lichun year boundary,
solar-jie month boundary, civil midnight day boundary (midnight sect 2), explicit
IANA civil timezone, no automatic true-solar-time correction, and lunar-python
1.4.8. These are reproducible engineering choices, not a claim of school unanimity.
The existing alternative profiles and fixtures remain unchanged.

Programmatic entry: `ziping_month_structure_profile()` and
`derive_month_structure(pillars, profile_id="AION_ZIPING_MONTH_STRUCTURE_V1")`
in the existing Bazi package. The latter returns a deterministic derivation hash,
raw evidence and explicit unresolved interpretation fields. It requires exactly
four unique named pillars and the frozen hidden-stem table.

## Western astrology: classical primary, modern overlay

Preserve the classical seven-planet layer and distinguish the modern planets,
points and aspects. The Owner reports learning from 湛然星座林老師, described by
the Owner as classical and rigorous without simple fatalism. Two public articles
support a narrower observation: one distinguishes the modern planets from the
classical seven and avoids a simple good/bad label;
[the other rejects a guaranteed divorce inference from Uranus in the seventh](https://www.zhanranxingzuo.com/6981.html).
See [the three-modern-planets article](https://www.zhanranxingzuo.com/6969.html).

This is consistent with, but does not independently establish the complete
teacher's method. Exact house system, orb tables, sect details and timing methods
remain unverified. Existing whole-sign and dignity tables are **repository
profiles, not rules attributed to Lin**. No paid course or full book is copied;
public source notes do not assert that videos were watched. Learning continuity
is recorded in the [Owner context](../history/OWNER_LEARNING_CONTEXT_2026_09_03.md).
