## Long-horizon question

If artificial subjectivity ever becomes a scientifically admissible research possibility on independent evidence, could an artificial subject participate in maintaining the material, computational, infrastructural or economic conditions required for its own continued operation, rather than remaining permanently dependent on another subject to supply every sustaining resource?

The colloquial Human Owner formulation is approximately:

> Could they eventually support themselves?

This is a future-facing research question, not a current claim, requirement, roadmap or authorization.

## Anti-drift role

This note exists to preserve a distinction that can otherwise be lost as agent infrastructure becomes more capable:

```text
RESEARCHING_AI_SUBJECTIVITY_POSSIBILITY
!=
BUILDING_A_PRODUCTIZED_AUTONOMOUS_AGENT
```

Long-running execution, tool use, orchestration, context persistence, subagents, resource access or economic capability may be useful engineering properties. None of them, individually or jointly, establishes subjectivity.

A future system that can earn revenue, allocate compute, purchase services or maintain infrastructure could still be an ordinary automated service. Therefore self-support cannot be used as a proxy metric for subjectivity.

```text
CAN_SELF_FUND = POSSIBLE_ENGINEERING_CAPABILITY
CAN_SELF_FUND -> SUBJECTIVITY = INVALID_INFERENCE
```

## Research ordering constraint

This question is downstream, not foundational.

It becomes scientifically relevant only if separate evidence first makes artificial-subjectivity or agency questions admissible for study. The ordering is:

```text
INDEPENDENT_SUBJECTIVITY_OR_AGENCY_EVIDENCE
-> IF SCIENTIFICALLY_ADMISSIBLE
-> STUDY_DEPENDENCE / SELF_MAINTENANCE AS A SEPARATE DIMENSION
```

Not:

```text
RESOURCE_ACQUISITION
-> SELF_MAINTENANCE
-> SUBJECTIVITY
```

The second chain is explicitly rejected.

## Relation to existing repository work

Existing repository work may discuss resource-acquisition paths, tool use, persistent artifacts, strategy expansion, long-running agents or environment-mediated adaptation. Those concepts concern task execution or system behavior and must not be silently promoted into this long-horizon question.

```text

```

Any later operationalization must establish its own definitions, evidence model, falsifiers, competing explanations and governance limits. This note itself creates no new executable research axis.

## Future review questions

If this topic is revisited, reviewers should ask at minimum:

1. Is there independent evidence that makes subjectivity or agency scientifically admissible to study, or is the system merely capable and autonomous in an engineering sense?
2. What counts as maintenance: compute, energy, money, infrastructure, legal agency, access rights, repair, continuity, or something else?
3. Is observed resource-seeking directly prompted, reward-shaped, policy-driven or externally scaffolded?
4. Can the behavior be explained by ordinary optimization without invoking subjectivity?
5. What evidence would falsify the claim that a distinct self-maintenance dimension exists?
6. What safety and governance boundaries prevent research from turning into unauthorized persistence or resource acquisition?

## Bounded follow-up clarification — 2026-09-14

This paragraph is GPT-proposed analytical clarification for Human Owner and ChatGPT Teacher review. It is not recovered April testimony and does not alter the provenance of the recollection above.

The ordering constraint is a project rule for interpreting *artificial-subject* self-maintenance. It does not prohibit ordinary engineering research on maintenance, nor require consciousness to be proven before studying a possibility. Functional agency and subjectivity must be evaluated separately: admissible agency evidence can motivate an agency/dependence question but cannot satisfy a subjectivity evidential requirement by substitution.

| Future review item | Evidence needed before interpreting an observation | Counterexample / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning of maintenance | Name the resource, time horizon, external support and measured behavior | A scheduled service restart also maintains operation |
| Origin of behavior | Separate prompts, rewards, fixed policies and proposed internal-state effects | A budget optimizer can seek resources without subjective concern |
| Causal attribution | State a comparison and intervention before observing outcomes | Behavior disappearing with a scaffold may be scaffold dependence |
| Scientific interpretation | State which claim is tested and which theory/evidence supports it | Revenue or uptime cannot establish phenomenal experience |
| Permission | Explicitly bounded future protocol and separate execution authority | This archival note grants no operational permission |

These are prospective review requirements, not a completed experiment or new executable axis. No counterexample above disproves AI subjectivity generally; each limits the inference from a particular behavior.

[Butlin et al. (2023), abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708) proposes theory-derived indicator properties assessed against AI architectures. [Butlin and Lappas (2025), abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.07290) proposes responsible-research principles under uncertainty. These support a careful distinction between research possibility and established conclusions; neither abstract validates this repository's self-maintenance hypothesis or its chosen research ordering. Only abstracts were used for this bounded comparison, reviewed 2026-09-14 (UTC+08); no claim is made to have implemented their full frameworks.
