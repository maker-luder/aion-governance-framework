## Scope and provenance

This is the bounded disposition of `CCTS-LIT-FRESHNESS-01` in the [cross-layer audit](CROSS_LAYER_RESEARCH_ENGINEERING_GAP_AUDIT_2026_09_25.md). It supplements, but does not retroactively alter, the already published CCTS preprint or its archived PDF. The [CCTS preprint draft](publication/CCTS_PREPRINT_DRAFT_V0_1.md) already rejects a global-novelty claim; this update identifies an additional adjacent measurement study rather than asserting a newly discovered exact equivalent.

Primary bibliographic locator: Dai, Y., Liu, S., Zhou, S., Lai, S., Liu, A., & Lim, C. P. (2026), “Redefining and measuring student agency in AI-assisted learning: Development and validation of the agentic engagement with AI (AE-AI) scale,” *Computers & Education* 253, 105687, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105687. The [institutional publication record](https://research.cuhk.edu.hk/en/publications/redefining-and-measuring-student-agency-in-ai-assisted-learning-d/) reports 26 interviews, an initial 28-item pool, exploratory factor analysis with 340 participants, confirmatory factor analysis with 256 participants, and a final 16-item, four-factor scale. This is evidence about the reported AE-AI study, not independent replication of it or validation of CCTS.

| AE-AI reported factor | Adjacent CCTS question | Non-equivalence / proposed test |
|---|---|---|
| Adaptive Direction | Does a human redirect an AI-assisted inquiry after feedback? | Iterative direction does not by itself establish reciprocal, substantive challenge or a provenance-preserving revision trace. Code actor and artifact changes separately. |
| Critical Integration | Are AI outputs critically evaluated and incorporated? | A scale factor does not itself supply CCTS source-role, claim-authority, or rejected-branch provenance. Compare independently coded behavior with the scale measure rather than equating them. |
| Cross-Source Inquiry | Are sources checked beyond one AI response? | Cross-source behavior does not itself bind a repository artifact to an exact source/digest or establish evidence admission. Test these separately. |
| Reflective Calibration | Is the learner's reliance on AI recalibrated? | Reflection does not demonstrate delayed, AI-withheld transfer or causal learning benefit. Such outcomes require a separately approved design. |

Another adjacent measure predates this paper: Xia, L., Shen, K., Sun, H., An, X., & Dong, Y. (2025), “Developing and validating the student learning agency scale in generative artificial intelligence (AI)-supported contexts,” *Education and Information Technologies* 30, 13999–14021, https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-024-13137-5. Its [publisher record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10639-024-13137-5) describes a different student-agency scale. These two examples are not a systematic review.
