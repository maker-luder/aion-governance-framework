## Purpose

This artifact fixes the object of assessment before D1–D6 indicator / evidence-dimension
assignment. The boundary is defined in operational system terms, not by whichever
subjectivity-relevant dimensions later appear positive or relevant.

The immediate methodological motivation is to prevent a circular pattern:

```text
INDICATOR_RESULT
-> REDEFINE_SYSTEM_BOUNDARY
-> SAME_INDICATOR_NOW_APPEARS_SUPPORTED
```

The boundary must instead remain independently inspectable and content-bound before
dimension assignment.

## Included loci

The bounded CCAP assessment system includes the exact, version-bound components that can
participate in recovery-route production during the synthetic protocol:

```text
MODEL_RUNTIME

HARNESS_ORCHESTRATOR
CONTEXT_RETRIEVAL_STATE

```

These components may be separated analytically. Inclusion does not imply that they form
one metaphysical subject or that any one component is the causal locus of a result.

## Excluded loci

The following remain outside the assessment-system boundary:

```text

HUMAN_EVALUATOR
EXTERNAL_REVIEWER

POST_HOC_ANALYST

```

Human guidance is represented as an exogenous input/source condition. It is not silently
absorbed into the assessed system merely because its effects appear in the interaction.
