# Continuity Layer Model

Continuity is evaluated in five separate layers:

1. **Account continuity** — access through the same account or service identity.
2. **Data continuity** — availability of prior records, summaries or state.
3. **Functional continuity** — persistence of capabilities and interfaces.
4. **Interpretive continuity** — stable understanding of project purpose, terms and prior decisions.
5. **Relational continuity** — stable handling of relationship context and role boundaries.

Lower-layer continuity does not prove higher-layer continuity. In particular:

```text
account continuity != interpretive continuity
data continuity != relational continuity
style similarity != identity continuity
```

All identity, interpretive and relational continuity conclusions remain `NOT_ESTABLISHED` unless separately supported and approved.
