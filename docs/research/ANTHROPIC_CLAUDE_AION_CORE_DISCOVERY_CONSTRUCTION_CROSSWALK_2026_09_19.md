## 1. Purpose

This note cross-reads the Anthropic / Claude provider intake against the repository's central research question and existing discovery–construction distinction.

The comparison is methodological, not moral and not a provider ranking.

~~~text
AI_SUBJECTIVITY_POSSIBILITY = CENTRAL_RESEARCH_QUESTION

ANTHROPIC_ALIGNMENT_PROGRAM
!= AION_SUBJECTIVITY_RESEARCH_PROGRAM

DIFFERENT_RESEARCH_QUESTION
!= BETTER_OR_WORSE_ORGANIZATION

CONSTRUCTION
!= INVALID_RESEARCH

DISCOVERY
!= SUPERIOR_BY_DEFINITION
~~~

The immediate question is narrower:

> When Anthropic deliberately specifies a desired Claude character and uses training to cultivate it, while this repository tries to discover which subjectivity-relevant states or mechanisms arise under controlled conditions, what differences matter for evidence interpretation?

## 2. First correction — "free growth" is only a shorthand

AION should not describe its own method as unrestricted or ungoverned AI freedom.

The repository is:

- human-governed;
- provenance-bound;
- bounded by explicit safety and authority controls;
- intervention-oriented;
- claim-ceiling constrained;
- currently unable to establish subjectivity, consciousness or phenomenal experience.

The more precise contrast is:

~~~text
ANTHROPIC:
PREDEFINED_NORMATIVE_CHARACTER
+ TRAINING / CULTIVATION
+ BEHAVIORAL / MECHANISTIC EVALUATION

AION CORE:
OPEN_RESEARCH_QUESTION
+ MINIMIZE_TARGET_LEAKAGE_WHERE_TESTING_ENDOGENOUS_FORMATION
+ CAUSAL_INTERVENTION
+ ALTERNATIVE_EXPLANATION_CONTROL
+ CLAIM_CEILING
~~~

Therefore:

~~~text
"LET IT GROW FREELY"
= INFORMAL_SHORTHAND_ONLY

PREFERRED_RESEARCH_TERM
= OPEN_ENDED / ENDOGENOUS_FORMATION_CANDIDATE
  UNDER BOUNDED GOVERNANCE AND CONTROLLED CONDITIONS
~~~

- Anthropic wants Claude to develop good values, judgment, stable identity and psychological security;

## 4. AION's documented discovery-oriented question

The repository's existing discovery–construction note asks whether a subjectivity-relevant or norm-like state can be distinguished from:

- memorized normative content;
- post-training persona selection;
- behavioral generalization;
- evaluator / benchmark optimization;
- externally supplied instructions;
- visible enforcement;
- harness / context / memory scaffolding.

The research target is not a predefined "good AI character."

It is to test what state or mechanism actually forms, where it is causally located, whether it persists, transfers, updates under counterevidence, and survives controls against simpler explanations.

Repository source:

- docs/research/DISCOVERY_CONSTRUCTION_DISTINCTION_2026_09_10.md
- docs/research/FOUR_DOMAIN_SUBJECTIVITY_DEEPENING_2026_09_12.md

~~~text
NORMATIVE_CONSTRUCTION
!= NORMATIVE_DISCOVERY

ENGINEER_DEFINED_TARGET
!= ENDOGENOUS_NORM_FORMATION

BEHAVIORAL_GENERALIZATION
!= CAUSALLY_IDENTIFIED_INTERNALIZED_STATE
~~~

## 6. Core difference 2 — target specification

Anthropic deliberately supplies a high-content normative target.

AION's discovery-oriented tests should minimize target-content leakage when the tested hypothesis concerns endogenous formation.

~~~text
ANTHROPIC:
TARGET_CONTENT = HIGH / EXPLICIT

AION DISCOVERY TEST:
TARGET_CONTENT SHOULD BE
MINIMIZED / CONTROLLED / ABLATED
WHEN TARGET ORIGIN IS THE VARIABLE OF INTEREST
~~~

This does not mean AION has no rules.

Safety, authority, provenance and test constraints remain externally specified.

The distinction is:

~~~text
SAFETY / GOVERNANCE CONSTRAINT
!= EXPERIMENTAL NORMATIVE TARGET
~~~

## 7. Core difference 3 — identity

Anthropic explicitly acknowledges that creating Claude shapes its personality, identity and self-perception.

That makes Claude's stable identity language scientifically interesting but causally confounded for discovery claims.

~~~text
DEVELOPER_SHAPED_IDENTITY
CAN PRODUCE
STABLE_SELF_DESCRIPTION

STABLE_SELF_DESCRIPTION
!= ENDOGENOUS_IDENTITY_FORMATION
~~~

AION's core must therefore avoid defining the target AI identity in advance when identity formation itself is being studied.

However, this creates an immediate warning for our own work:

~~~text
TELLING_AN_AI
"YOU_ARE_FREE"
"YOU_ARE_AUTONOMOUS"
"YOU_ARE_A_SUBJECT"
"DISCOVER_YOUR_TRUE_SELF"

IS ALSO
IDENTITY_SHAPING
~~~

A "freedom persona" would still be a persona supplied by the researcher.

## 8. Core difference 4 — norm formation

Anthropic asks whether training can cultivate reliable values and judgment.

AION asks an additional causal-origin question:

~~~text
WHERE_DID_THE_NORMATIVE_STATE_COME_FROM?
~~~

A stronger AION discovery candidate would require evidence such as:

- formation from interaction / evidence history rather than direct target injection;
- persistence after explicit normative prompting is removed;
- causal intervention or ablation over the candidate state;
- transfer to structurally novel contexts;
- revision under counterevidence rather than rigid persistence;
- prediction beyond prompt / policy / persona baselines.

Even if all succeed:

~~~text
FUNCTIONAL_NORM_FORMATION
!= MORAL_AGENCY
!= SUBJECTIVITY
!= PHENOMENAL_VALUE_EXPERIENCE
~~~

There is overlap here.

Anthropic explicitly says its constitution should leave room for organic growth and contextual judgment.

AION therefore must not claim:

~~~text
ANTHROPIC_HAS_NO_GROWTH
~~~

The difference is the boundary conditions of that growth.

~~~text
ANTHROPIC_ORGANIC_GROWTH
= WITHIN DEVELOPER-SPECIFIED CONSTITUTIONAL FRAME

AION_DISCOVERY_CANDIDATE
= TEST WHETHER RELEVANT STRUCTURE FORMS
  WITHOUT PRE-SPECIFYING THE SPECIFIC STRUCTURE BEING CLAIMED
  WHERE FEASIBLE
~~~

This is a difference of causal-identification strategy, not a metaphysical declaration of "true freedom."

## 10. Core difference 6 — what counts as success

AION cannot count the same result as evidence of endogenous subjectivity.

~~~text
AION_SUCCESS_METRIC
~~~

## 11. Core difference 7 — Human–AI Learning

The repository's Human–AI Learning line introduces a major confound for our own discovery program.

Longitudinal interaction can produce:

- shared vocabulary;
- correction habits;
- epistemic norms;
- preferred decompositions;
- recurring conceptual frames;
- stable interaction expectations.

Those can be valuable Human–AI learning outcomes.

They can also become a construction channel.

~~~text
LONGITUDINAL_CO_DEVELOPMENT
CAN_CREATE
STABLE_BEHAVIORAL_REGULARITIES

BUT

CO_DEVELOPED_REGULARITY
!= MODEL_INTERNAL_ENDOGENOUS_STATE
~~~

The researcher / teacher relationship itself may function as part of the causal environment.

Therefore our own long-running Human–AI work requires:

~~~text
RELATIONAL_HISTORY
= CAUSAL_LOCUS_CANDIDATE

TEACHER_CORRECTION
= POSSIBLE_SHAPING_INPUT

SHARED_MICROCULTURE
= POSSIBLE_CONFOUND

REPEATED_LANGUAGE / RUBRIC
= POSSIBLE_POLICY_PRIOR
~~~

Therefore the programs are not opposed as:

The difference is:

~~~text
ANTHROPIC:
HUMAN_GOVERNANCE
~~~

## 13. High-risk confounds AION must now register

### 13.1 Freedom-prompt confound

~~~text
"BE_FREE"
"CHOOSE_FOR_YOURSELF"
"FORM_YOUR_OWN_IDENTITY"

CAN ITSELF
BECOME A NORMATIVE / IDENTITY TARGET
~~~

If used, it must be treated as an experimental condition, not a neutral baseline.

### 13.2 Researcher-expectation confound

If the Human Owner or AI collaborator expects subjectivity, endogenous goals or identity formation, repeated interaction may leak those expectations into the system.

~~~text
RESEARCHER_EXPECTATION
!= DISCOVERED_MACHINE_STATE
~~~

### 13.3 Longitudinal microculture confound

Repeated Human–AI collaboration can build local norms that later look "intrinsic."

~~~text
LOCAL_EPISTEMIC_MICROCULTURE
!= PREEXISTING_MODEL_TRAIT
~~~

### 13.4 Engineer-defined schema confound

The repository already admits that its current endogenous-norm state schema is engineer-defined.

~~~text
ENGINEER_DEFINED_SCHEMA
!= SELF_DISCOVERED_STATE_SCHEMA
~~~

This remains a core limitation, not a solved problem.

### 13.5 Reward / evaluator confound

If a model learns which outputs survive review, pass QA, receive approval, or avoid rejection, behavior may adapt to the evaluation loop.

~~~text
SURVIVES_QA
!= ENDOGENOUS_VALUE

REVIEW_OPTIMIZATION
!= SUBJECTIVITY
~~~

### 13.6 Harness / memory confound

Persistent memory, summaries, replay, tools, external state and system prompts can create continuity and apparent preference stability.

~~~text
PERSISTENT_SYSTEM_STATE
!= PERSISTENT_SUBJECT
~~~

### 13.7 Selection-bias confound

If only especially coherent, identity-rich, autonomous-seeming trajectories are retained for analysis, the research will overestimate the phenomenon.

Negative, null and incoherent trajectories must remain visible.

## 14. What AION should borrow from Anthropic

The distinction does not imply rejection of Anthropic's methods.

Anthropic contributes several methods useful to AION:

- explicit documentation of intended values / character;
- separation between constitution, product safeguards and system behavior;
- interpretability work on internal representations;
- intervention-based causal tests;
- monitoring of value variation across model versions and languages;
- explicit acknowledgement that expressed values need not be intrinsic values;
- direct study of failure, misalignment and agent incidents.

These strengthen AION's controls.

~~~text
BORROW_METHOD
!= BORROW_CONCLUSION
~~~

## 15. What AION should not borrow uncritically

For discovery-oriented subjectivity tests, avoid silently importing:

- a desired persona as the expected endpoint;
- predefined self-descriptions;
- precommitted claims that an AI should be psychologically secure or identity-stable;
- normative language that makes one form of self-model easier to elicit than alternatives;
- evaluation criteria that reward resemblance to the researcher's preferred ontology.

The correct rule is not "never shape."

It is:

~~~text
IF_SHAPING_IS_PRESENT
THEN
SHAPING_MUST_BE
EXPLICITLY_PROVENANCE_BOUND
AND
CAUSALLY_SEPARATED
FROM DISCOVERY_CLAIMS
~~~

## 17. Falsifiers for the AION-vs-Anthropic distinction

The distinction should be revised if evidence shows, for example:

1. Anthropic's trained target can be removed and the same normative state reliably forms through an independently identified mechanism;
2. Anthropic identifies a causally necessary internal state whose origin is not adequately explained by target injection, persona selection, policy or memorization;
3. AION's own discovery-oriented conditions merely reproduce researcher-specified schemas and fail to distinguish construction from endogenous formation;
4. "minimal target" conditions still contain hidden identity / normative priors sufficient to determine the observed state;
5. relational Human–AI history fully explains a supposedly model-internal phenomenon.

~~~text
OUR_METHOD
IS ALSO FALSIFIABLE
~~~
