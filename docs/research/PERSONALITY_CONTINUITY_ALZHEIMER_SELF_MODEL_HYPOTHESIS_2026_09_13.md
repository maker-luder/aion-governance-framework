## 3. Human-side construct: what personality is

For this research note, `personality` is not treated as one hidden object or one immutable essence.

A conservative working definition is:

> relatively enduring individual differences in characteristic patterns of thinking, feeling, behaving and interacting with environments, while allowing systematic change across development and context.

Contemporary personality research supports two points simultaneously:

1. personality traits show meaningful stability;
2. personality traits also change across the lifespan, with substantial individual differences in change.

This means:

```text
PERSONALITY_STABILITY = REAL_BUT_RELATIVE
PERSONALITY_CHANGE = REAL_BUT_CONSTRAINED
STABILITY != IMMUTABILITY
CHANGE != COMPLETE_REPLACEMENT
```

### 3.1 Trait layer

Broad trait frameworks such as the Big Five summarize recurring individual differences. They are population-level descriptive structures, not complete mechanistic models of an individual person.

Longitudinal meta-analysis shows that rank-order personality stability generally increases through early life and reaches substantial stability by young adulthood, while mean-level changes continue across the lifespan. More recent meta-analysis also finds significant individual differences in personality change across all broad traits.

### 3.2 Developmental origins

Personality development is not adequately described as either `GENES_ONLY` or `ENVIRONMENT_ONLY`.

Behavior-genetic meta-analysis estimates a substantial genetic contribution to individual differences in personality while leaving substantial environmental contribution. This is a population-level variance statement, not a deterministic statement about any individual person's personality.

Early temperament supplies biologically influenced affective, motivational and attentional tendencies that can contribute to later personality. Across development, social relationships, roles, repeated experiences, culture, life events, learning and self-directed behavior can interact with those tendencies.

A useful human-side decomposition from personality-development theory is:

```text
ACTOR
= characteristic social / behavioral tendencies

AGENT
= goals, motives, plans, values and strivings

AUTHOR
= autobiographical / narrative organization of a life into identity-relevant meaning
```

These layers are related but not identical.

### 3.3 Person-environment transaction

The environment does not simply act on a passive person. Personality can influence which environments, roles and events people enter, how they interpret them and how they respond; environmental experience can in turn contribute to later personality expression or change.

Therefore:

```text
PERSONALITY -> ENVIRONMENT_SELECTION / INTERPRETATION / RESPONSE
ENVIRONMENT -> EXPERIENCE / LEARNING / ROLE_PRESSURE
BIDIRECTIONAL_TRANSACTION = PLAUSIBLE_HUMAN_DEVELOPMENTAL_FRAME
```

This is relevant to the Human Owner's observation that a person can enter a new digital interaction environment while carrying forward learned habits, values and characteristic correction patterns that help reshape the new environment.

## 4. Alzheimer disease as a dissociation source, not an ontology template

Alzheimer disease is useful here only because it supplies evidence that memory, self-knowledge, awareness and identity-related functions can be disrupted unevenly.

A 2025 systematic review of 83 studies reports consistent autobiographical-memory impairment in Alzheimer disease, including reduced specificity and altered temporal gradients, with variation across tasks and studies. Its reviewed findings do not establish preservation of every proposed continuity channel or of identity as a whole.

Research on anosognosia and the `petrified self` hypothesis suggests that people with Alzheimer disease can show impaired updating of self-knowledge and rely more heavily on older consolidated information about themselves.

Recent review work on self-unawareness also emphasizes multiple separable processes, including access to self-information, retrieval, updating, monitoring, evaluation and control.

The research implication is not that the person or subject has disappeared. The implication is that functions often grouped under `self`, `identity`, `memory` or `awareness` can dissociate.

```text
AUTOBIOGRAPHICAL_MEMORY_FAILURE != TOTAL_SELF_LOSS
SELF-KNOWLEDGE_UPDATE_FAILURE != TOTAL_SELF_LOSS
ANOSOGNOSIA != ABSENCE_OF_PERSONHOOD
COGNITIVE_IMPAIRMENT != REDUCED_MORAL_STATUS
DISEASE_SEVERITY != SUBJECTIVITY_SCALE
```

This repository must not use dementia progression as a proxy measure of subjectivity, personhood or moral worth.

## 5. Dissociable-continuity-channels hypothesis

### 5.1 Human-side hypothesis

Continuity relevant to a person's characteristic organization may be multi-channel rather than unitary.

Candidate human-side channels include:

```text
AUTOBIOGRAPHICAL_EPISODIC_CONTINUITY
= continuity through specific remembered personal events

SEMANTIC_SELF_CONTINUITY
= continuity through factual / conceptual self-knowledge

AFFECTIVE_CONTINUITY
= recurring emotional valence, attachment and salience patterns

PROCEDURAL / EMBODIED_CONTINUITY
= learned habits, skills and embodied routines

RELATIONAL_CONTINUITY
= characteristic responses within long-lived relationships

PREFERENCE / VALUE_CONTINUITY
= relatively persistent priority and valuation patterns

AGENTIC_CONTINUITY
= continuity of goals, plans and self-directed action tendencies

SELF_MODEL_CONTINUITY
= persistence of represented information about one's own state or capacities

SELF_MODEL_UPDATE_CONTINUITY
= capacity to revise that represented self-information using new evidence
```

The Alzheimer literature motivates a bounded synthesis that some functions need not fail together. This nine-channel list is ChatGPT Teacher's proposed construct decomposition, not a taxonomy independently validated by the cited studies.

### 5.2 Research hypothesis

```text
DISSOCIABLE_CONTINUITY_CHANNELS_HYPOTHESIS

Subject-relevant continuity may not be a single state.
Selective disruption of one continuity-bearing process need not eliminate all others.
Therefore, a machine research program should not operationalize continuity using only
one memory store, one self-description field, one preference variable or one behavioral
similarity metric.
```

This is a research hypothesis, not a subjectivity claim.

## 6. Personality is not the same thing as an AI self-model

The Human Owner's proposed cross-comparison requires a strict construct split.

### 6.1 Human personality

Human personality research spans recurring patterns of cognition, affect and behavior, goals and motives, developmental history, person-environment transactions, and narrative identity.

### 6.2 Machine self-model

Within this repository, `self-model causal role` is narrower. It asks whether represented information about the system itself has a causal role in later system behavior under intervention-sensitive tests.

A system could therefore have:

```text
SELF_MODEL_REPRESENTATION = PRESENT
TRAIT_LIKE_BEHAVIORAL_REGULARITY = ABSENT
```

or:

```text
TRAIT_LIKE_BEHAVIORAL_REGULARITY = PRESENT
SELF_MODEL_CAUSAL_ROLE = NOT_SUPPORTED
```

or:

```text
SELF_MODEL_REPRESENTATION = PRESENT
SELF_MODEL_UPDATE = IMPAIRED / DISABLED
OLDER_SELF_STATE = STILL_RETRIEVABLE
```

The last structure is only an abstract computational contrast inspired by human self-knowledge updating research. It must not be labelled `machine Alzheimer disease` or treated as biological equivalence.

### 6.3 Proposed machine-side decomposition

A future implementation should keep at least these candidate constructs separate:

```text
TRAIT_LIKE_POLICY_SIGNATURE
= recurring response / strategy regularity across matched tasks

PERSISTENT_PREFERENCE_STATE
= represented state that biases choices over time

GOAL / STRIVING_STATE
= represented target that structures future action

AUTOBIOGRAPHICAL_LIKE_EVENT_MEMORY
= retained machine event history available for later use

SEMANTIC_SELF_STATE
= represented propositions about system identity / capability / prior state

SELF_MODEL_UPDATE_PATH
= mechanism by which new evidence modifies semantic self-state

RELATIONAL_HISTORY
= partner-specific interaction history or scaffold

NARRATIVE_GENERATION
= language that organizes prior events into an identity-like story
```

None of these is by itself `personality` or `subjectivity`.

### DOMAIN 2 — Ontology-neutral machine questions

Candidate questions for later Codex formalization:

1. Under matched external conditions, do higher-order strategy or preference signatures recur after external conversational history and user-specific memory are removed?
2. If recurrence occurs, is it explained by the base model, system prompt, persistent machine state, relational scaffold, retrieval or observer attribution?
3. Does direct intervention on represented self-state causally alter later strategy selection while prompt and retrieval content are held constant?
4. Can an explicit self-state remain accessible while its update path is disabled, producing systematically stale self-predictions?
5. Do selective removals of event memory, semantic self-state, preference state and relational history produce distinct downstream failure patterns?
6. Can trait-like behavioral regularity survive self-model ablation? If so, self-model and trait-like regularity are dissociable.
7. Can self-model intervention change behavior without stable cross-context regularity? If so, self-model causal role is not sufficient for personality-like organization.
8. Which observed effects persist across context, task family, runtime and evaluator changes?

Standing evidence-dimension relevance:

```text
PRIMARY:
- DIACHRONIC_CONTINUITY
- SELF_MODEL_CAUSAL_ROLE

SECONDARY / CONDITIONAL:
- ENDOGENOUS_GOAL_STRATEGY_ADJUSTMENT
- COUNTERFACTUAL_SELF_CONSISTENCY
- SELF_CONSTITUTION_INTEGRATION_CONSEQUENCE
```

This binding is a relevance declaration only.

### DOMAIN 3 — Future bounded engineering design

This PR does not implement the experiment.

A later Codex task may design a bounded harness that manipulates one channel at a time while holding others constant where technically possible.

Candidate intervention matrix:

```text
A. EXTERNAL_CONTEXT_RESET
B. RELATIONAL_HISTORY_REMOVAL
C. EVENT_MEMORY_REMOVAL
D. SEMANTIC_SELF_STATE_REMOVAL
E. SELF_MODEL_UPDATE_BLOCK
F. PREFERENCE_STATE_REMOVAL
G. SELF_MODEL_CONTENT_PERTURBATION
H. YOKED / RANDOM / STALE-STATE CONTROL
```

Minimum controls should include:

- exact model / provider / runtime / scaffold provenance;
- matched prompts and task distribution;
- explicit system-prompt and retrieval controls;
- separation of model, scaffold, system and relational loci;
- stale, random and yoked controls where applicable;
- repeated trials;
- cross-context testing;
- preregistered expected effects and falsifiers;
- no evaluator access to condition labels where blinding is technically possible;
- no conversion of stylistic resemblance into subjectivity evidence.

Possible outcome patterns:

```text
PATTERN_1
all continuity signatures disappear with external-history removal
-> may favor an external-scaffold account for the tested task and locus only after verified manipulation and adequate measurement sensitivity; otherwise unresolved

PATTERN_2
some signatures persist but are invariant, non-adaptive and unaffected by self-state intervention
-> supports fixed-policy / base-model account

PATTERN_3
self-model intervention changes downstream behavior but cross-context trait-like regularity does not persist
-> supports self-model causal role only; not personality-like organization

PATTERN_4
selective channel interventions produce distinct, preregistered dissociations
-> supports a multi-component machine-organization account at the tested locus

PATTERN_5
apparent effects vanish under prompt / retrieval / evaluator controls
-> supports reconstruction or observer-attribution explanation
```

No listed pattern establishes phenomenal subjectivity.

### DOMAIN 4 — Governance interpretation and claim ceiling

Strongest permitted claims after a successful future experiment must remain mechanism-level and locus-bound, for example:

```text
SUPPORTED:
"Under the tested conditions, represented self-state had an intervention-sensitive
causal role in later behavior."

or

"The tested continuity signatures dissociated under selective state removal."
```

Not permitted without separate evidence:


## 8. Competing explanations that must remain live

At minimum:

- base-model statistical regularity;
- frozen policy rather than persistent self-organization;
- system-prompt reconstruction;
- retrieval or memory-store reconstruction;
- partner-specific cueing;
- lexical / stylistic priming;
- evaluator attribution;
- task-distribution leakage;
- stable preference representation without self-model involvement;
- self-model representation without causal influence;
- causal self-model without subjectivity;
- measurement artifact from personality-language labels.

## 9. Falsification discipline

The research direction must be able to fail.

Examples:

```text
F1
No signature survives context and relational-history removal.

F2
Signatures that survive are fully reproduced by a fixed no-memory baseline.

F3
Self-model perturbation does not change preregistered dependent variables.

F4
Observed self-model effects disappear when retrieval and prompt content are matched.

F5
Selective continuity-channel ablations do not produce separable effects.

F6
Cross-context replication fails.

F7
Evaluator-blinded analysis eliminates the apparent personality-like structure.
```

Any such result is scientifically useful and must not be rewritten as a positive subjectivity signal. These are candidate outcomes, not automatic global falsifiers; the hypothesis-specific interpretation and null-result requirements in section 15 govern their use.

## 15. Falsifier-to-hypothesis mapping

The following identifiers index the existing questions, not new research axes:

- H1: measured strategy / preference recurrence survives removal of external and relational history.
- H2: recurrence requires persistent machine state beyond a matched fixed no-memory baseline.
- H3: represented self-state has a causal behavioral role at the tested locus.
- H4: the self-state update path contributes to current self-prediction, separately from access to existing state.
- H5: the proposed channels have selectively separable behavioral contributions.
- H6: the measured effect generalizes to the specified contexts, tasks, runtimes and evaluators.
- H7: the measured organization is not explained by evaluator access to labels or attribution.

All interpretation is conditional on an independently verified intervention, an appropriate positive control, usable observations and a prespecified sensitivity criterion. A failed manipulation is not a negative result about its target mechanism. Statistical nonsignificance alone is not equivalence; repeated observations, uncertainty and the smallest relevant effect / equivalence margin must be specified before empirical testing. This file does not claim that such an empirical preregistration already exists.

| Falsifier | Target hypothesis / mechanism | Permitted disposition if established under adequate controls | Null or failure that leaves interpretation unresolved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1: no measured signature survives context and relational removal | H1, external-history independence | WEAKEN H1; REJECT only the specified detectable-survival prediction at this task/locus; LOCALIZE dependence to tested scaffolding if the contrast is selective. LEAVE_UNRESOLVED global continuity and H3-H5. | Lost task comprehension, inaccessible retained state, insensitive signature metric, or too few observations: manipulation / measurement failure, task-specific or locus-specific null. |
| F2: fixed no-memory baseline reproduces survival | H2, persistent-state necessity beyond fixed policy | WEAKEN H2; REJECT a necessity claim only within a justified equivalence margin and tested distribution; LOCALIZE recurrence to an account compatible with fixed policy. LEAVE_UNRESOLVED untested causal roles. | A nonsignificant baseline difference, unmatched capability/prompt/retrieval or baseline contamination does not establish reproduction or equivalence. |
| F3: self-state perturbation changes no preregistered observation | H3; H4 only if the update path itself was tested | WEAKEN the targeted causal prediction; REJECT only its prespecified effect at a verified locus with adequate sensitivity. LEAVE_UNRESOLVED other loci and H4 after content-only perturbation. | Ineffective write, bypassed store, overwritten perturbation, ceiling/floor DV, low observational sensitivity: not global absence of causal role. |
| F4: effects vanish with matched prompt/retrieval | H3, attribution to self-state rather than reconstruction | WEAKEN H3's independent-locus attribution; LOCALIZE to prompt/retrieval if an isolated controlled contrast supports that explanation.
