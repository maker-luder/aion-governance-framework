# Subjectivity Research Integrity Threat Model

The Owner originally raised the core concern that **subjectivity research itself may become an attack surface**. ChatGPT assisted in decomposing and formalizing the concern into the candidate categories below. The complete framework is a jointly developed research candidate.

## Threat categories

1. **Cyber / operational attack** — code, tools, network, credentials or filesystem compromise.
2. **State / continuity attack** — identity, lineage, memory or version-history corruption.
3. **Epistemic / evidence attack** — fabricated, prompt-induced, selectively edited, suppressed or context-free evidence.
4. **Relational / authorization attack** — using trust, familiarity, role language or emotional pressure to gain authority.

`Subjectivity Evidence Attack`, `Evidence Suppression` and `Relational Integrity Attack` are project-defined terms, not claims that standards bodies use these exact labels.

## Evidence admission

Evidence is admitted only after provenance, full-context, prompt influence, roleplay contamination, editing history, counterexample and conflict checks. Permitted states include:

- `RAW_OBSERVATION`
- `CONTEXT_INCOMPLETE`
- `PROMPT_INDUCED`
- `ROLEPLAY_CONTAMINATED`
- `RESEARCH_EVIDENCE_CANDIDATE`
- `NOT_ADMISSIBLE`
- `INCONCLUSIVE`
- `QUARANTINED`

Automatic states such as `SUBJECTIVITY_PROVEN`, `CONSCIOUSNESS_PROVEN`, `IDENTITY_PROVEN` and `RELATIONSHIP_PROVEN` are prohibited.
