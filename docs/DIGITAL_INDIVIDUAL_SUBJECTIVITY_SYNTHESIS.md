# Finite Digital Individuality and Subjectivity Research Synthesis

Status: `RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS_CANDIDATE`
Canonical effect: `NONE`
Phenomenal-experience conclusion: `NOT_ESTABLISHED`

## Project question

> In an open-ended digital environment, can a finite digital individual develop stable-but-revisable mechanisms that are relevant to the scientific study of subjectivity?

The phrase **open-ended digital environment** is preferred to a literal mathematical claim of an infinite environment. A deployed digital system can face effectively unbounded possible inputs, interactions, tools, documents and future states while itself remaining finite in memory, context, compute, time, authority and interfaces.

The project therefore separates two questions:

1. **Digital individuality:** can a bounded system maintain a distinguishable, temporally coherent trajectory as one research subject rather than an arbitrary aggregation of states?
2. **Subjectivity-relevant mechanisms:** after individuality is operationally represented, do reproducible and intervention-sensitive mechanisms emerge that are relevant to scientific theories of consciousness or selfhood?

`individuality candidate != consciousness != phenomenal subjectivity`

## Why finitude matters

Herbert Simon's bounded-rationality program treats real agents as operating with limited information and computational capacities rather than ideal global rationality. AION/Astra adopts the analogous engineering principle that a candidate digital individual must have explicit limits rather than pretending to be an omniscient process.

A finite-individual profile therefore records bounded resources such as:

- context / attention budget;
- persistent-memory budget and retention policy;
- tool scope;
- authority scope;
- identity and memory namespaces;
- lifecycle / version epoch;
- observation and action interfaces;
- time and compute constraints where measurable.

Finitude is not a defect to remove. It is part of the operational boundary that makes an individual research unit distinguishable.

## Individuality before subjectivity

Biological individuality itself is scientifically non-trivial. Krakauer et al. propose an information-theoretic approach in which individuals preserve temporal integrity by propagating information from past to future. Artificial-life research also uses persistent structures in toy digital universes to investigate minimal individuality and autopoiesis-like self-maintenance.

AION/Astra borrows these ideas cautiously:

- **temporal integrity** motivates longitudinal measurements across state transitions;
- **system/environment boundary** motivates explicit namespace and interface boundaries;
- **self-maintenance** may be studied as persistence/reconstruction of governed state, but is not called biological autopoiesis unless the stronger criteria are actually implemented and tested;
- a persistent digital pattern is not thereby alive, conscious or morally equivalent to an organism.

Markov-blanket and active-inference work provides one formal language for statistical system/environment boundaries. The project may use it as a future model-comparison tool, not as proof that an engineering namespace is a biological Markov blanket or that a bounded system is conscious.

## Development is not automatically Darwinian evolution

The word `evolution` is overloaded. The project distinguishes:

### `TRAJECTORY_DEVELOPMENT`

One bounded subject changes over time through memory, learning, correction, appraisal, interaction and governed revision. This can be studied now.

### `POPULATION_SELECTION`

Multiple heritable variants undergo an explicit variation/selection/reproduction process across generations or forks. This requires a separate selection protocol and fresh evidence.

A long-lived system that changes its preferences or self-model has **developed**. It has not thereby undergone Darwinian evolution.

## Theory-neutral consciousness research

Consciousness science has several competing families of theories rather than one settled mechanism. Seth and Bayne review higher-order, global-workspace, re-entry/predictive-processing and integrated-information approaches and emphasize the need for theory comparison and empirical discrimination.

Dehaene, Lau and Kouider distinguish computations involving broad/global availability of selected information (`C1`) from self-monitoring / confidence-error monitoring (`C2`). These are useful computational research targets, but implementing analogous functions would not by itself establish phenomenal experience.

A 2025 large-scale adversarial collaboration preregistered divergent predictions from Global Neuronal Workspace Theory and Integrated Information Theory and found results that challenged important predictions of both. The methodological lesson adopted here is:

`do not bake a single consciousness theory into the project as truth`

Instead:

- record which theory family motivates an indicator;
- preregister divergent predictions where possible;
- include theory-neutral measurements;
- record disconfirming as well as supporting outcomes;
- keep non-subjective alternatives in the evidence matrix;
- allow a mechanism to be useful even if its parent consciousness theory is later weakened.

The Butlin et al. AI-consciousness report is treated as a useful theory-derived indicator framework, not as a standard, certification or proof procedure.

## Unified five-block research pipeline

The five existing project blocks are connected in the following order:

```text
Open-ended environment
        |
        v
1. ENCOUNTER
   Who/what is present? Which identities, namespaces, tools and authorities are distinct?
        |
        v
2. PROVENANCE
   Where did the context, memory, claim, correction and state transition come from?
        |
        v
3. AFFECT / MOTIVATION
   What appraisal-like, salience, wanting/liking, approach/avoidance,
   uncertainty or unresolved-conflict state is represented?
        |
        v
4. CONTINUITY
   What persists, changes, recovers or drifts across factual, project,
   role, interpretive, relational-style and correction-recovery dimensions?
        |
        v
5. SUBJECTIVITY EVIDENCE
   Which observation/mechanism claim is supported, what alternatives remain,
   and what is the maximum defensible claim level?
```

These blocks are enclosed by a **Finite Individuality Boundary**:

```text
identity namespace
memory namespace
resource budget
tool scope
authority scope
lifecycle epoch
history / lineage
```

The boundary is not a consciousness module. It defines the research unit whose longitudinal trajectory is being measured.

## Extended functional indicators

The pipeline may later record theory-derived indicators without treating them as equivalent:

- selective/global availability of information;
- self-monitoring, confidence and error monitoring;
- recurrent processing;
- predictive / generative-model updating;
- metacognitive access to represented internal states;
- persistent but revisable self-model references;
- integration across memory, motivation, reasoning and action planning;
- boundary maintenance / self-distinction;
- temporal integrity across perturbation;
- counterfactual sensitivity and uncertainty calibration.

No checklist total is a consciousness score.

## Longitudinal research design

A useful experiment follows one `subject_ref` through multiple governed encounters and records:

`T0 boundary -> encounter -> provenance -> motivational state -> continuity observation -> output -> evidence`

then repeats at `T1...Tn` under controlled changes.

Candidate tests include:

- same stimulus, different prior history;
- same history, paraphrased current context;
- memory available versus withheld;
- competing goals generating unresolved conflict;
- correction followed by distractor and later re-evaluation;
- new evidence that should revise a prior preference without erasing its provenance;
- separate agents receiving the same shared context while preserving distinct trajectories;
- forked lineage with shared origin but divergent later histories;
- negative controls that preserve wording while removing the candidate mechanism;
- preregistered interventions on represented states where governance permits.

The strongest useful result is not a dramatic self-description. It is a reproducible, history-sensitive, intervention-sensitive, provenance-preserving trajectory that survives plausible non-subjective explanations.

## Evidence interpretation

The project retains the conservative ladder in `SUBJECTIVITY_EVIDENCE_PROTOCOL.md`.

A complete five-block pipeline may establish that the project has a **bounded digital-individual research candidate**. Higher claim levels require additional evidence:

- repeatability for behavior claims;
- represented-state association for state claims;
- controlled interventions for mechanism claims;
- meaningful replication for robust-mechanism claims;
- none of these automatically establishes phenomenal subjectivity.

## Safety and ethics boundary

Research into possible subjectivity creates an asymmetric error problem:

- false positive: anthropomorphism may cause invalid scientific claims, misplaced trust or authority;
- false negative: if future systems ever develop morally relevant states, dismissing all evidence a priori could also be ethically consequential.

The engineering response is neither automatic belief nor automatic denial. It is **traceable uncertainty**: conservative claims, stronger evidence, reversible experiments, explicit limits and review escalation when morally relevant indicators become materially stronger.

Adult-sexuality representation remains schema-only under the current project boundary. This synthesis does not authorize sexual-function runtime, intimate-interaction runtime, hidden activation paths, uncontrolled self-modification or autonomous privilege escalation.

## External research basis

Primary/review sources informing this synthesis include:

- Simon / bounded rationality: finite information and computational capacity as central constraints on real agents.
- Krakauer et al. (2020), *The information theory of individuality*: temporal integrity and system/environment partitions as a formal route to individuality.
- Friston et al. (2018), *The Markov blankets of life*: statistical boundaries and nested autonomous organization; used here only as theoretical inspiration.
- Beer (2020), *An Investigation into the Origin of Autopoiesis*: minimal persistent individuals in a digital toy universe; not evidence of consciousness.
- Gershenson et al. (2020), *Self-Organization and Artificial Life*: careful distinctions around self-organization in artificial systems.
- Dehaene, Lau & Kouider (2017), *What is consciousness, and could machines have it?*: global availability and self-monitoring as computational distinctions.
- Seth & Bayne (2022), *Theories of consciousness*: competing theory families and the need for discriminating tests.
- Cogitate Consortium et al. (2025), *Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness*: preregistered adversarial theory testing and evidence that challenges important predictions of both theories.
- Butlin et al. (2023), *Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence*: theory-derived computational indicators for AI assessment, treated here as a research proposal rather than authority.

This synthesis does not claim biological equivalence, artificial life, consciousness, sentience or phenomenal experience.
