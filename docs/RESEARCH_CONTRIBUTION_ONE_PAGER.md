# Research Contribution — One Page

> Public research synthesis of existing repository evidence. This document introduces no new subjectivity claim, canonical state, deployment authority, or research result.

## Research question

AION asks how possible artificial subjectivity can be studied without confusing memory-like behavior, continuity-like behavior, simulation, implementation, relationship language, or researcher interpretation with evidence that subjectivity exists.

The central methodological problem is therefore not only what a system can do, but how researchers prevent plausible interpretation from silently becoming project history, identity fact, memory truth, or authority.

## Core contribution

This repository develops a **human-governed, provenance-first, claims-separated and auditable research framework** for studying identity, continuity, memory, interpretation drift, bounded capability and artificial-subjectivity hypotheses while preserving the distinction between implementation evidence and ontological conclusions.

The main contribution is to treat the **research process itself as an auditable object**. Important claims are expected to retain source, attribution, temporal position, transformation lineage, authority status and revision history rather than collapsing observations, interpretations and approved state into one narrative.

## Stable whitepaper method inheritance

The retained integrated-whitepaper lineage treats `v0.14.23` as the stable/frozen integration baseline and `v0.14.24` as a later internal research candidate. The stable method is not replaced merely because `main` uses a more compact operational evidence-card format.

The whitepaper method preserved by the research reconciliation includes:

- `FOUR_STAGE_INFERENCE`;
- six subjectivity-inference-relevant evidence dimensions:
  1. causal boundary;
  2. diachronic continuity;
  3. self-model causal role;
  4. endogenous goal / strategy adjustment;
  5. counterfactual self-consistency; and
  6. states consequential to the system's own constitution / integration;
- alternative explanations;
- causal intervention, ablation and counterfactual testing;
- cross-context robustness;
- replication;
- provenance;
- admissibility;
- claim scope; and
- result–mechanism separation.

These are evidence and inference disciplines, not a subjectivity score. The whitepaper reconciliation states that even the strongest fourth-stage disposition is at most a `PLAUSIBLE_CANDIDATE`, not an established subjectivity conclusion.

## Methodological contributions

1. **Provenance-first research records** — source, speaker, event time, record time, transformation and authority remain distinguishable.
2. **Claims separation** — observation, self-report, inference, hypothesis, evidence candidate and approved/canonical state are different epistemic objects.
3. **Identity and lineage isolation** — shared models, schemas, genesis, similar outputs, copied state or relationship do not silently merge subjects, instances or research forks.
4. **Governed recall** — topic-cued retrieval is treated as candidate recall, not truth; identity, access, provenance, conflict, supersession and relevance gates determine eligibility.
5. **Relationship-authority separation** — familiarity, trust, dependency, persistence or relational language cannot elevate privileges or silently authorize state changes.
6. **Fail-closed writeback** — retrieved or generated material cannot silently rewrite durable canonical state.
7. **Explicit non-claims** — software behavior, continuity, memory, embodiment, bounded execution and successful tests are not treated as proof of consciousness or subjectivity.

## Engineering and experimental evidence

The repository materializes these methods through bounded and auditable engineering artifacts, including:

- a governance pipeline from Context Intake through Risk Gate, Policy Check, bounded tool routing, Writeback Gate and Audit Sink;
- identity, continuity, memory-recall and research-integrity governance components;
- a bounded non-canonical executable runtime candidate;
- release verification, public-tree scanning and component-level test infrastructure;
- a deterministic Bazi domain used as a constrained example for provenance, transformation and capability-boundary testing;
- a minimal Recall-Gate contrast experiment comparing naive cue overlap with governed recall on synthetic records.

The Recall-Gate experiment demonstrates only that the implemented gate blocks or quarantines the tested classes of invalid cue-matching records. It does not establish subjective memory, model-level psychological effects, identity continuity or whole-system validity.

## What this framework enables

The framework is intended to make research failures inspectable rather than narratively absorbed. A reviewer can ask, for example:

- where a claim originated and how it changed;
- whether a retrieved record was eligible, current and authorized;
- whether an identity or research fork was silently merged;
- whether relationship language was incorrectly converted into authority;
- whether generated interpretation crossed a writeback boundary;
- whether a reported result is implementation evidence, research inference or an approved conclusion.

This makes the repository useful not only as a collection of controls, but as an **auditable failure recorder for long-horizon AI research**.
