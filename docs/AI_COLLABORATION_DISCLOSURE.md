## Main-transition authority

AI review, candidate implementation authority, research autonomy, CI/QA success, or prior Human Owner authorization for another action **MUST NOT** be inherited as permission to merge into `main`.

```text
CANDIDATE_SCOPE_APPROVAL != MERGE_APPROVAL
AUTONOMOUS_RESEARCH_PERMISSION != MAIN_TRANSITION_AUTHORITY
QA_PASS != MERGE_APPROVAL
AI_REVIEW != HUMAN_OWNER_MERGE_APPROVAL
SILENCE != CONSENT
```

A `main` merge requires fresh, action-specific, target-specific, explicit Human Owner approval. Missing or contradictory approval evidence fails closed to `HOLD`.
