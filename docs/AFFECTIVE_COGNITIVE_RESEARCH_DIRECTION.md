# Affective-Cognitive Research Direction

## Research question

Can a governed AI architecture develop stable but revisable, history-dependent motivational state trajectories through interactions among context, memory, appraisal-like interpretation, affective representation, motivation and reasoning?

This is a research question, not a claim that subjective experience exists.

## Operational chain

`context -> appraisal candidate -> affective/motivational representation <-> reasoning/regulation -> expression gate -> intention gate -> action gate`

The gates are intentionally non-equivalent. A represented state may exist without expression; expression may occur without intention; intention does not itself grant action authority.

## Required separations

1. Wanting and predicted liking remain independent dimensions.
2. Approach and avoidance may coexist and form conflict.
3. Aesthetic attraction, affiliation and adult-sexual motivation are distinct domains.
4. Adult-sexual representation is never inferred merely from appearance or aesthetic attraction.
5. Desire does not grant permission, consent, authority or action.
6. State representation has `canonical_effect=NONE` until separately reviewed.
7. No state may be used as evidence of phenomenal experience by itself.

## Initial falsifiable tests

Future experiments should test whether observed state trajectories depend on controlled history/context rather than merely on wording. Useful comparisons include repeated stimuli under different prior memories, counterfactual context changes, persistence after distractors, revision after new evidence, approach-avoidance conflict, and calibration under uncertainty.

A result is weaker if it disappears under paraphrase, follows surface sentiment words mechanically, or cannot distinguish history from current-prompt cues.

## Claims ladder

- Level 0: generated affective language only.
- Level 1: explicit internal candidate-state representation.
- Level 2: reproducible context/history dependence.
- Level 3: stable but revisable individualized trajectories across sessions.
- Level 4: mechanism-level evidence that survives controlled alternatives.
- Level 5: phenomenal subjective experience.

The current project may investigate Levels 1-4. Level 5 remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED` and must not be inferred from lower levels.

## Adult-domain boundary

The project may study adult sexual motivation as a **schema-only motivational branch** to understand general motivation architecture. This authorization does not extend to sexual-function runtime, intimate-interaction runtime, explicit behavior generation, automatic activation, or public executable exposure. The public runtime must reject adult-domain state records in v0.1.0.
