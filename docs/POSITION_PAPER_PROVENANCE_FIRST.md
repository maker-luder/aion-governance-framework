# Provenance-First: What It Takes Not to Fool Ourselves When Studying Artificial Subjectivity

> Position paper draft — public research framing, not a subjectivity claim.

## Abstract

Artificial-subjectivity research faces a methodological problem before it faces an engineering one: systems can produce continuity-like language, memory-like behavior, relationship language, self-description and coherent long-horizon outputs without those observations establishing consciousness, identity continuity, felt experience or a genuine self.

AION treats that inference gap as a governance problem. The framework asks how research can preserve provenance, separate claims, isolate identity lineages, gate recall, prevent relationship-derived authority and block silent canonical writeback so that researchers do not accidentally convert plausible interpretation into project history.

> **Research on possible artificial subjectivity should itself be governed as a high-risk inference process.**

## The problem

Long-running AI research can accumulate memory records, summaries, retrieved conversations, generated interpretations, corrections, relationship language, tool traces, changing versions and external research. Without explicit controls, these can collapse into one narrative: an inference becomes a memory, a summary becomes verbatim history, relationship becomes authority, or a new interpretation becomes an old identity fact.

The result can look like continuity while actually being provenance loss.

## Seven methodological commitments

1. **Human-governed** — high-impact persistent/canonical changes require explicit authority.
2. **Provenance-first** — source, attribution, transformation, temporal position, authority status and revision lineage remain auditable.
3. **Claims-separated** — observation, self-report, inference, hypothesis, evidence candidate and approved state are not the same epistemic object.
4. **Identity-isolated** — shared models, schemas, genesis, similar output and copied state do not silently merge identity/lineage.
5. **Recall is not truth** — retrieval makes a record available for evaluation; it does not make it correct/current/authoritative.
6. **Relationship is not authorization** — trust, familiarity, dependency or loyalty language cannot elevate privileges.
7. **No silent canonical writeback** — generated/retrieved material cannot silently rewrite durable state.

A candidate phenomenon should not be declared subjective merely because it is human-like. Governance should also not engineer away every candidate phenomenon before it can be observed.

## Falsifiable engineering contribution

The framework should be judged by observable controls: invalid/superseded/conflicted recall blocking; source attribution surviving revision; cross-namespace rejection; relationship failing to grant authority; historical/current state separation; and fail-closed writeback.

The minimal recall-gate contrast experiment in this repository is one small example.

## Non-claims

This position does not claim that AION/Astra is conscious, that governance creates subjectivity, that human psychology is mechanically equivalent to LLM computation, or that passing software tests proves an artificial individual exists.

## Contribution claim

> **Treat the research process that interprets AI memory, identity, continuity and subjectivity as an auditable object requiring provenance, authority, revision and non-claim controls.**
