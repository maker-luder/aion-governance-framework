## Main-branch isolation

```text

MAIN_WRITE_AUTHORITY = NOT_GRANTED
MAIN_EFFECT = NONE
CANONICAL_EFFECT = NONE
```

No research-branch result, passing test, external citation, reproduced behavior, high self-report score, mechanistic indicator, or multi-stream evidence may silently modify, merge into, or become canonical on `main`.

Any future transfer from research branch to `main` requires a separate Human Research Owner decision, fresh review against the then-current `main`, explicit provenance review, QA/CI verification, and a dedicated integration path. Wholesale branch merge is not implied by this charter.

## Research freedom inside the branch

Within the research branch, the workbench may:

- create new research labs and synthetic fixtures;
- preserve failed, negative, null, contradictory, or inconclusive results;
- compare external theories and public research projects without treating them as authority;
- implement clean-room tests inspired by externally observed methods where licensing and provenance permit;
- perform ablation, perturbation, control, replication, false-positive, and construct-validity experiments;
- extend provenance, continuity, memory, subjectivity-evidence, affect/motivation, encounter, embodiment, and research-integrity investigations;
- add research-only CI and reproducibility checks;
- refine terminology when earlier wording risks claim inflation or source-attribution drift.
