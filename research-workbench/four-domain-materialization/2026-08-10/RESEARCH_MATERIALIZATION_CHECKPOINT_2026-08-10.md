## Purpose

This checkpoint materializes the research structure that emerged from the 2026-08-10 IQC/reconstruction cycle without wholesale-merging the reconstruction branches and without promoting any result to `main`, canonical state, or runtime.

The research branch is used here as a governed synthesis surface: reviewed results, negative findings, salvage decisions, open gaps, and methodological corrections are recorded as research material. Separate reconstruction branches remain the implementation/evidence sources for their own candidates.

## Current research shape

The material now separates three functional levels that must not be collapsed under the word “metacognition”:

```text
LEVEL 1 — REPRESENTATION
    represented self-state / self-other state

LEVEL 2 — FIRST-ORDER FUNCTION
    bounded predictive self-model
    prediction -> COMMIT / DEFER
    matched functional ablation

LEVEL 3 — SECOND-ORDER COMPUTATION
    estimate the performance/reliability of a first-order mechanism
    use only valid prior evidence
    test causal contribution under matched controls
```

Current disposition:

```text
LEVEL_1_REPRESENTATION = IMPLEMENTED_AS_RESEARCH_CANDIDATES
LEVEL_2_FIRST_ORDER_FUNCTION = IMPLEMENTED_AND_CI_VERIFIED_ON_RESEARCH_BRANCH

```

The open Level-3 gap is not an implemented module. A partial external-agent build attempt was independently rejected and removed from its branch head; only reviewed conceptual lessons are retained.

## Evidence-oriented reconstruction line

The reconstruction cycle converged on a recurring pattern:

```text
HUMAN / PSYCHOLOGICAL LABEL
    -> DO NOT ASSUME

RESEARCH QUESTION
    -> OPERATIONAL UNIT
    -> EVIDENCE + PROVENANCE
    -> MEASUREMENT CONTRACT
    -> CAUSAL / FUNCTIONAL TEST
    -> NON-CLAIM BOUNDARY
```

Examples:

```text
relationship score
    -> bounded encounter evidence

continuity score
    -> continuity evidence lineage

development score
    -> longitudinal change evidence

body-state story
    -> embodiment capability primitives

motivational-dynamics story
    -> motivational signal primitives
```

This does not imply that every future construct should become an “evidence” module. The operational mechanism must still be chosen from the actual research gap.
