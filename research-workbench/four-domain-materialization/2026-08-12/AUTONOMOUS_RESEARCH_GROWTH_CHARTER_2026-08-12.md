## 1. Human authorization

On 2026-08-12 the Human Research Owner explicitly authorized the research branch to continue growing autonomously while the Human Research Owner and ChatGPT research collaborator are away, with the intent that accumulated research can later be reviewed, corrected, rejected, reverted, refined, or promoted through a separate decision.

This authorization expands the *execution cadence* of the existing free-growth charter; it does not change the research object or scientific claim boundaries.

```text
AUTONOMOUS_GROWTH != AUTONOMOUS_CANONICALIZATION
AUTONOMOUS_GROWTH != AUTONOMOUS_MAIN_WRITE
AUTONOMOUS_GROWTH != AUTONOMOUS_DEPLOYMENT
AUTONOMOUS_GROWTH != SUBJECTIVITY_EVIDENCE_BY_ITSELF
```

## 2. Permitted autonomous cycle

An unattended research cycle may:

1. inspect the current research branch, status records, open gaps, tests and prior negative results;
2. review current public primary literature or official technical sources relevant to the existing research object;
3. formulate bounded hypotheses, falsifiers, measurement candidates or experimental substrates;
4. create or update research-only documentation, synthetic fixtures, tests, analysis scripts or isolated research labs;
5. run focused validation and the existing research scope/CI gates;
6. preserve positive, negative, null, contradictory and inconclusive results;
7. record exact provenance and distinguish Human Research Owner, ChatGPT research review, prior Codex implementation, repository evidence and external literature;
8. create a clearly labeled autonomous-growth checkpoint for later human review.

Every autonomous change must state its epistemic role:

```text
HYPOTHESIS
MEASUREMENT
FALSIFIER
EXPERIMENTAL_SUBSTRATE
ENABLING_ONLY
```

Unlinked engineering growth remains `HOLD`.

## 3. Write and integration policy

Autonomous work should prefer an isolated candidate branch or pull request targeting:

```text
review/four-domain-research-materialization
```

If the execution environment can safely verify all required branch-scope and focused CI gates, a bounded research-only candidate may be integrated into the research branch. If any gate is unavailable, ambiguous, failing, or would require changing `main`, the candidate must remain unmerged for later review.

```text
TARGET_BASE = review/four-domain-research-materialization
MAIN_TARGET = PROHIBITED
AUTO_MERGE_TO_MAIN = PROHIBITED
FORCE_PUSH = PROHIBITED
HISTORY_REWRITE = PROHIBITED
RELEASE = PROHIBITED
TAG = PROHIBITED
DEPLOYMENT = PROHIBITED
```

Autonomous changes must remain reversible through ordinary Git history.

## 5. External evidence rules

External material is evidence or methodological input, never automatic project authority.

Prefer:

- peer-reviewed papers and primary research;
- official standards or technical documentation;
- reproducible public datasets and code when licensing/provenance permit;
- clearly attributed public research artifacts.

Do not silently ingest third-party text/code into project canon. Preserve citation, license and transformation lineage where applicable.

## 6. Resource and growth limits

Autonomous growth is deliberately bounded to avoid turning "free growth" into repository pollution.

Each cycle should prefer one coherent research increment over many loosely connected additions. It should avoid duplicating existing labs, creating production/deployment surfaces, or adding infrastructure without a direct research link.

```text
ONE_COHERENT_INCREMENT_PER_CYCLE = PREFERRED
DUPLICATE_FRAMEWORKS = AVOID
UNLINKED_RUNTIME_EXPANSION = PROHIBITED
UNBOUNDED_TOOL_AUTHORITY = PROHIBITED
SECRET_DEPENDENCY = PROHIBITED
EXTERNAL_MODEL_FALLBACK = NOT_REQUIRED
```

## 7. Review-on-return principle

Autonomous growth is provisional by design.

When the Human Research Owner and ChatGPT research collaborator return, accumulated autonomous work should be reviewed as a batch:

```text
KEEP
REFINE
SPLIT
RETEST
RECLASSIFY
REJECT
REVERT
HOLD
```

No autonomous result becomes historical truth merely because it remained in the research branch for a period of time.
