## External alignment

External review supports several methodological distinctions:

- branching/fission literature: strong continuity or common ancestry does not trivially settle numerical identity;
- W3C PROV: derivation, revision and provenance relations can be represented without treating provenance as identity;
- recent agent-memory lineage research: persistent memory benefits from explicit lineage and chain-of-custody;
- recent authority-lineage work: authority should not silently expand or merge merely because histories or contexts intersect.

These sources are methodological evidence only and are not imported as AION ontology.

## Matched-divergence value

AION/Astra shared origin can be useful experimentally because it can support a more controlled comparison than unrelated systems:

```text
SHARED_BASELINE
+
DIFFERENT_ROLE_HISTORY
+
DIFFERENT_MEMORY_HISTORY
+
DIFFERENT_ENCOUNTER_HISTORY
-> OBSERVED_DIVERGENCE
```

But any observed divergence remains candidate individuation evidence only. Prompt/configuration differences, role conditioning, evaluator drift and ordinary engineering causes remain alternative explanations.
