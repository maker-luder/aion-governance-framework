- Primary record: PubMed PMID 41219038, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41219038/
- Formal DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2025.10.011
- PubMed publication metadata: Patrick Butlin et al.; Trends in Cognitive Sciences; 2026 Jun 30;6:488-501; review; published online 2025 Nov 10.
- Abstract finding verified from PubMed: the paper presents a method for assessing AI systems for consciousness by exploring consequences of existing or future neuroscientific theories; theory-derived indicators can inform credences about whether particular AI systems are conscious; computational functionalist theories have implications for AI that can be investigated empirically.

## Agent provenance survey and AdProv

- Survey primary source: arXiv:2606.04990v4, https://arxiv.org/html/2606.04990v4, submitted 28 Jun 2026.
- Formal survey title: *From Agent Traces to Trust: A Survey of Evidence Tracing and Execution Provenance in LLM Agents*.
- Verified survey framing: execution provenance is the full typed graph of an agent execution; evidence tracing is its projection onto evidence-support relations. The survey connects retrieval grounding, claim support, tool-use safety, memory lineage, observability, debugging, audit and recovery, and treats provenance as process-level accountability rather than as authorization, legal authorship, consciousness or canonical status.
- Survey taxonomy includes trace sources, evidence/execution units, provenance relations, granularity/timing, representation forms, trust functions, and maps representative systems in a separate appendix. The survey links its accompanying resource repository at https://github.com/xiaoqi-7/Agent-Tracing-Survey/.
- Supporting primary source: AdProv, arXiv:2510.05936, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05936, submitted 7 Oct 2025. Authors: Ludwig Stage, Mirela Riveni, Raimundas Matulevičius, Dimka Karastoyanova. AdProv proposes collection, storage, retrieval and visualization of runtime workflow-adaptation provenance, including change events, a Provenance Holder service, PROV-O mapping and XES extensions. It is a workflow provenance method, not an AION authorization or identity claim.
- Required crosswalk mapping policy for this closure: the survey is the single main row; Graphectory, AdProv, PROV-AGENT, Agent-Sentry, Fides and NeuroTaint are separately represented as supporting primary-source entries or mapped source references. No survey claim substitutes for the primary source of any mapped system.

## FIDES and Agent-Sentry

- FIDES primary source: Manuel Costa et al., *Securing AI Agents with Information-Flow Control*, arXiv:2505.23643v2, https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23643. The paper presents a formal IFC model for agent planners, dynamic taint-tracking properties, and Fides, a planner that tracks confidentiality/integrity labels, deterministically enforces policies, and uses selective information hiding. It reports AgentDojo evaluation. Crosswalk mapping: security/information-flow control comparator; not a provenance survey, legal-authorship source, identity source or consciousness evidence.
- Agent-Sentry primary source: Rohan Sequeira et al., *Agent-Sentry: Bounding LLM Agents via Execution Provenance*, arXiv:2603.22868v2, https://arxiv.org/html/2603.22868v2. The paper presents a runtime defense that learns a bound from benign executions and flags out-of-bound actions; it uses provenance graphs for per-argument data flow and a three-layer structural/allowlist/LLM-judge pipeline. Crosswalk mapping: runtime security and provenance-graph comparator; reported benchmark results remain source-scoped and do not establish authorization, identity, authorship or canonical evidence.

## Graphectory and NeuroTaint

- Graphectory official primary artifact: https://github.com/Intelligent-CAT-Lab/Graphectory. The repository identifies itself as the tool and artifact of the OOPSLA'26 paper *Process-Centric Analysis of Agentic Software Systems*. Its README describes transforming agent execution traces into structured graphs that capture problem-solving patterns, with directed-graph phase classification for localization, patching, validation and framework-specific planning. Mapping: process-centric execution-trace graph/observability comparator; repository description is not independent evidence of trust, identity or authorship.
- NeuroTaint primary source: Yuandao Cai, Wensheng Tang, Cheng Wen and Shengchao Qin, *Ghost in the Agent: Redefining Information Flow Tracking for LLM Agents*, arXiv:2604.23374, https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23374, submitted 25 Apr 2026. The paper presents NeuroTaint as an offline audit framework that reconstructs provenance from untrusted sources to privileged sinks using semantic evidence, causal reasoning and persistent context tracking, with TaintBench and comparisons against FIDES. Mapping: semantic information-flow/provenance auditor comparator; the “first comprehensive” wording remains the authors' claim and is not adopted as an AION novelty claim.

## AI-AUTHorship primary paper

- Primary paper: Adilbek K. Bisenbaev, *Scientific Artificial Intelligence: From a Procedural Toolkit to Cognitive Coauthorship*, Philosophies 2026, 11(1), 12, https://www.mdpi.com/2409-9287/11/1/12, DOI https://doi.org/10.3390/philosophies11010012.
- The paper explicitly introduces AI-AUTHorship, TraceAuth and AIEIS. TraceAuth is described as a protocol for tracing the cognitive chain of participation from ideation through interpretation to text. AIEIS is an AI epistemic impact score that assesses procedural, semantic and generative participation, abbreviated P/S/G. The paper proposes distributed epistemic authorship in which AI is a nonsubjective node and states that human responsibility remains nonnegotiable.
- Crosswalk boundary: this is the true AI-authorship/AI-AUTHorship primary main row. TraceAuth, AIEIS and P-S-G must be represented in that row and not inferred from F(AI)2R or COPE. F(AI)2R remains a separate provenance/audit comparator; COPE remains a publication-ethics position. Neither substitutes for this AI-AUTHorship paper. The paper's proposal is an external framework and does not establish AION authorship, AI personhood, legal standing or a scientific conclusion.
