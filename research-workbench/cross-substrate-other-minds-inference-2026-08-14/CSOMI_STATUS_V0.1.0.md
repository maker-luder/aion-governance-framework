## Claims supported by this milestone

The milestone supports the **method claim** that other-minds reasoning can be represented as a defeasible comparison of explicit hypotheses with evidence channels, alternatives, disanalogies, controls, falsifiers, scope and credence updates. It also supports the design of a bounded `CAPABILITY` record for false-belief/ToM-like task performance under declared synthetic controls.

The milestone does not support a claim that any AI or AION system has a mind, subjectivity, phenomenal consciousness, sentience, identity continuity, moral status, legal status, autonomous authorship or governance authority.

## Claims weakened or rejected by the method

The method rejects direct jumps from language similarity, human-like behavior, a single indicator, self-report, memory persistence, a successful test, a passing CI workflow or a stored identity label to subjectivity. It weakens any cross-substrate inference that hides biological/technical disanalogies, treats human-derived constructs as automatically transferable, omits non-mental alternatives, or reports sensitivity and specificity without data.

The method also rejects treating `CAPABILITY` as equivalent to `SCIENTIFIC_CONCLUSION`. The machine-enforced rule is:

> `RESEARCH_TOPIC != CAPABILITY != SCIENTIFIC_CONCLUSION`

## Claims that remain uncertain or held

The following remain unresolved: whether any theory-derived indicator transfers validly across substrates; whether any target system has a causal/mechanistic organization relevant to phenomenal consciousness; whether self-report is causally tied to a privileged state in a non-human substrate; whether memory persistence is more than storage or reconstruction; whether ToM-like task success reflects mental-state representation; and whether independent replication would survive cross-context, operator and substrate-relevant controls.

`Sensitivity=NOT_ESTIMATED` and `specificity=NOT_ESTIMATED` for every evidence channel. No quantitative diagnostic performance is implied by this design packet.
