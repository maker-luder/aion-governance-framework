## Transformation limits

The oscillation threshold of two direction reversals is a synthetic contract parameter, not a validated scientific or governance threshold. `OSCILLATORY` means only that the supplied event metadata contains two direction reversals; it does not mean a real system or person is unstable. `STALE`, `CONTRADICTORY`, and `UNKNOWN` are evidence-status labels supplied to the ledger, not externally verified facts.
