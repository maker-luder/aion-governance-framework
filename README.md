# AION Governance Framework

> **[繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) | English**
>
> **Historical termination record — 2026-08-20:** the AION / Astra project work loop was terminated following explicit Human Owner approval and the recorded architecture, evidence, provenance, and privacy review. That event remains historically authoritative for its event time. It was not a scientific conclusion about artificial subjectivity, consciousness, identity continuity, or phenomenal continuity, and it did not authorize automatic restart.
>
> **Current repository standing:** after termination, a small number of explicitly authorized, bounded maintenance increments were merged into `main`. Those maintenance events do not rewrite the termination event and do not constitute a project or research restart.

AION is a human-governed, provenance-first research framework for studying identity, continuity, memory, research integrity, and the **possibility of artificial subjectivity** without treating implementation behavior as proof of subjectivity. Astra is the distinct engineering and research workbench used to materialize and test bounded candidates.

The central research question remains scientific and unresolved. Agent runtimes, harnesses, execution substrates, provenance systems, and evidence tooling are **research instrumentation** for making observations and mechanisms inspectable; they are not subjectivity evidence merely because they are sophisticated or successful.
