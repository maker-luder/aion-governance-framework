# Bazi Interpretation Boundary

Deterministic calendar facts are stored separately from interpretation
candidates. An interpretation must identify its method, supporting facts,
assumptions, alternatives, confidence and owner-review state. It may be
superseded but not overwritten.

`SCORE != TRUTH`, `INTERPRETATION != REALITY_EVIDENCE`, and
`INTERPRETATION != AGENT_SELF_MEMORY`. No LLM interpretation is part of this
candidate.
