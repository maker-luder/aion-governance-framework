# Classical volume intake and rule provenance

The twelve main volumes of the Wikisource **Siku Quanshu transcription of San
Ming Tong Hui** are now retained at exact revision IDs. This supersedes only the
previous index-only intake, not a historical method decision. It is not a claim
that the transcription is fully proofread against the scans, includes every
preface/variant, or is the unique correct edition.

Author: Wan Minying (萬民英), Ming dynasty. Edition: 四庫全書本.
Historical text is public-domain material; Wikisource editorial/transcription
contributions retain their applicable CC BY-SA terms. Each manifest URL links
to the exact revision; its history identifies contributors. No Apache-only
relicensing of this third-party textual layer is asserted.
