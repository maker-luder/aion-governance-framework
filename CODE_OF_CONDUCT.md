# Code of Conduct

Participation must remain respectful, evidence-oriented and compatible with the repository's human-governed research purpose.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, doxxing, attempts to expose private project material, pressure to bypass safety or approval gates, false claims of certification or subjectivity, and attempts to convert relational language into access authority.

Report security-sensitive concerns privately through the process in `SECURITY.md`. Ordinary technical disagreements should be documented with reproducible evidence and without personal attacks.
